## Modes

While any mode is playing, the current mode's display name (from `MODE_LABELS` in `copy.js`) appears as a small persistent uppercase caption at top-center of the experience screen (`top:16, left:'50%', transform:'translateX(-50%)'`, plain text, no background) — always on for the whole session, does not fade, distinct from `TutorialText` (`top:calc(44% + 24px)`) and `BreathLengthControl` (`top:16,left:16`).

### Guided Breathing: Tutorial
- Internal id `'tutorial'`. Top card on Select Mode and the default (`selectedMode` default, `TESTING_DEFAULTS.selectedMode`). A saved `'basic'` is migrated to `'tutorial'` on load.
- A 3-part flow driven by a **Continue** button (bottom center, its bottom 12 px above the tops of Home/Restart; rounded like the mode cards, colored and pressed like Start). Part texts are centered vertically between the mode name and the top of Continue (`TutorialText` `region`). State is in `tutorialRef` (`part`: screen / rounds / practice / count / paced), timers in `tutorialTimersRef`, and copy in `TUTORIAL` (`copy.js`). There's no 10 s mode explanation. Part texts show at the top (the explanation's spot and size) over the blank cover, and the corner Home/Restart stay visible.
  1. **Part 1**: Continue fades the text out. "Inhale and / move the sliders up." fades in (2 s), with the sliders and the Morph only (`tutorialArt 'morph'`: no landscape/background) starting to fade in `TUTORIAL_REVEAL_LAG_MS` 1 s after it. Three Inhale/Exhale rounds follow (`updateDiagonalSequence`, either layout, no B texts). Then practice (`updateTutorialPractice`, inhale-start/exhale-start detection, 8% deadband):
     - "You got it!…" for one breath.
     - `PRACTICE_FREE_BREATHS` 1 free breath, then "Notice how…" from the next inhale start to the exhale start.
     - `PRACTICE_AFTER_BREATHS` 2 more breaths, then the cover comes back (`tutorialGoBlank`).
  2. **Part 2**: Continue mounts the art's landscape/background (`tutorialArt 'background'`, no gates), reveals the scene and turns counting on (slider-driven). Count cycle 1's `'cycleDone'` (10 breaths) goes blank.
  3. **Part 3**: one screen. A small **Test Bell** pill (`navPillStyle`, the Home/Restart look) sits between "…or exhale." and "Above and below the sliders…": the copy has a `TEST_BELL_MARK` line that `TutorialText` replaces with its `insert` prop, fading with the text. Each tap calls the engine's `testBell()`: the Inhale/Exhale bell at full (Exhale) level, restarting if still ringing, wired straight to the output so it plays while the Part screen has the rest of the audio muted. Its Continue (`startTutorialPaced`) sets the 6-6 pace (`spawnIntervalRef` 12, inhale/exhale refs 6) and mounts the gates (`tutorialPaced`, `startOnInhale`, plus `SlowingDownPaceRingsD` for D). It then reveals the scene with "Relax and prepare to inhale.". After `TUTORIAL_PREPARE_MS` 3 s, the countdown runs on `TutorialText`'s sub line (`subVisible`): 3, 2, 1, 1 s each, 0.3 s fades, set `TUTORIAL_SUB_GAP_PX` 15 px lower than the text. The prepare text fades out during "1"; "Inhale" shows on the countdown line as `gatesEnabledRef` turns on, which starts the first paced Inhale, bell and target, and fades out after `COUNTDOWN_START_MS` 2 s (the same sequence as Slowing Down, `runPreparedStart(true, …)`). From then on it behaves like Slowing Down after its ramp, with no captions (`pacedCueStageRef 'done'`). Bells, lit slider labels and the pace pair use the Slowing Down branch. `TUTORIAL_DONE_AFTER_MS` 24 s after "Inhale" (the 3rd paced Inhale), "Good job! The tutorial is done…" (`TUTORIAL.done`) fades in for `TUTORIAL_DONE_SHOW_MS` 10 s, then fades out.
- **Covered screens** (also Box Breathing's and Slowing Down's opening screens): while the scene is covered, the sliders ignore touches (`slidersDisabledRef` → `useTouchSlider`'s `disabledRef`, which also stops a touch already in progress). The sliders keep working while the cover fades in (`coverFading`, until the `SCENE_REVEAL_MS` timer) and the layer's pointer events follow `slidersLive`. Going blank before Parts 2 and 3 fades all audio out (`setMuted` on the engine's master gain, over `SCENE_REVEAL_MS`), and it fades back in as the scene is revealed. Once the cover is fully up, the sliders are remounted (`sliderResetKey`) at their defaults (left 0, right 1 = full Exhale), so they fade back in from there rather than where they froze.
- **Skip Tutorial**: on the Part 1 and Part 2 screens only, a smaller outline-only button (16 px, `skipRef`) sits in Continue's usual spot and Continue moves up by its height + 12 px (`showSkip`, `tutorialScreenId`). `handleTutorialSkip` mounts the full art, turns counting on and runs `startTutorialPaced({ showDone: false })`: Part 3's paced start without the closing "Good job!" text.
- **Reminder**: in Parts 1 (practice) and 2, `TUTORIAL_REMIND_MS` 5 s without slider movement fades the text to "Remember to / move the sliders / with your breath.". The next movement fades back to the step's text, or to nothing, and the flow carries on. The usual 10 s idle re-show is off in this mode.

### Basic
- Hidden from the Select Mode picker (replaced by Tutorial); still fully implemented.
- Morph + environment, no Gates.
- Display label: **"Breathe at Your Own Pace"** (internal mode id remains `'basic'` everywhere in the code — only the display string changed).

### Paced Breathing
- Full experience with Gates at a fixed 12-second interval.
- Hidden from the Select Mode picker (`SelectModeScreen.jsx`'s `OPTIONS` array no longer lists it), same pattern as Shape Options A/C being hidden from the Art Options picker — the mode, its display label, its tutorial copy, and all of `App.jsx`'s `mode === 'timed'` routing stay fully implemented and reachable in code, just not selectable from the UI.
- **BreathLengthControl**: a small slider + value + label at top-left (`BreathLengthControl.jsx`) that lets the user adjust the breath interval. Hidden (opacity 0) until 2 seconds after Text D fades in, then fades in over 2 seconds. Resets to 12s and hides again when the user returns to this mode. Paced-Breathing-only — Slowing Down no longer uses it (see that section).
- **Gate spawn timing**: gates start disabled (`gatesEnabledRef.current = false`). They enable only when `showGatesText` fires (i.e., when Text C appears — after Text A and B complete, or immediately if the user skips past them). This means the road shows ties but no gate rings until Text C.

### Box Breathing
- **Opening (Tutorial-style, `usesScreens`)**: no mode explanation, A/B intro or count tutorial (the Tutorial covers those). Start/Restart shows `MODE_SCREENS.box` at the top over the blank cover with Continue (`showTutorialScreen('box')`), with a Test Bell (as in Tutorial Part 3) after "…different bell." that plays the Hold bell (`BELL_HOLD`) at full level. Continue (`handleTutorialContinue`) reveals the full scene and audio, turns counting on (slider-driven, palette/sound change every 5 breaths), then `runPreparedStart(false, …)`: "Relax and prepare to inhale." for `TUTORIAL_PREPARE_MS` 3 s, then 3, 2, 1 on the sub line (no "Start"; the text fades out during "1"), and `startBoxArt()` as "1" ends — the first Inhale, with the gates/pace art, bells, lit labels and the 2 captioned cycles below.
- Display label: **"Guided Breathing: Box Breathing"** (internal mode id remains `'box'` — only the display string changed, in `MODE_LABELS.box`).
- 4-phase box breath: Inhale → Hold-in → Exhale → Hold-out. Each phase duration = `spawnIntervalRef.current` seconds (`BOX_STEP_SECONDS` 6, in `breathPace.js`), split into `BOX_COUNTS` 4 counts of 1.5 s.
- **Gate mechanics**: `spawnSeries(type)` spawns N=`BOX_COUNTS` (4) gates simultaneously at `SPAWN_Z=−6` with even spacing, so they arrive one count (1.5 s) apart. Speed = `Math.abs(SPAWN_Z) / spawnIntervalRef.current` u/s (1 u/s at 6 s). Last gate (`isLast=true`) triggers the next series when it crosses z=0; type alternates inhale↔exhale. First gate (`isFirst=true`) fires `onFirstGate(type)`; last gate fires `onLastGate(type)` — both pre-triggered at z=−speed×2 so text can start fading before the gate reaches z=0.
- **Gate files**: `GatesBoxBreathingA.jsx` (torus rings), `GatesBoxBreathingB.jsx` (cube-style), `GatesBoxBreathingC.jsx` (torus + sphere) — matched to shape options A, B, and C respectively (pool: 28 slots per file). Shape option D also uses `GatesBoxBreathingC.jsx` (same visible gates as Option C) — Box Breathing is the one mode where Option D shows visible Gates, see Gate geometry section above. Shape Option E uses `GatesBoxBreathingHeadlessE.jsx` instead — same timing, zero meshes — since Option E never shows visible Gates in any mode (see Gate geometry section above); it pairs with `BackgroundB` (rings) and `StarFieldE` (hold-phase starfield flare) for pacing.
- **No road ties** — Box Breathing does not render railroad ties.
- **Caption layout**: "Inhale"/"Exhale" eases in over the first 90% of its phase and stays up through the Hold after it. "Hold" (`TutorialText` `subText`, absolutely positioned on the line below so the caption doesn't move) fades in over `HOLD_FADE_IN_S` 1 s as the Hold starts, with no per-second pulse. Both ease out together over the Hold's last 10% (`boxAlphas` in `TutorialText.jsx`, × the per-round `ROUND_ALPHA`).
- **Tutorial coaching sequence**: 2 full box-breath cycles then text hides. Managed by `bbCycleRef` (counts completed exhale-phase "last gate" events) and `bbTutorialActiveRef` in App.jsx. Uses `BB_TEXT_LEAD_MS = 1600ms` for `transitionBoxText` timeout (vs `FADE_TRANSITION_MS = 2000ms` used elsewhere) — ensures text starts appearing when the gate is at z=−0.6, just before the steep emissive ramp at z=−0.5 to z=0.

### Slowing Down
- **Opening (Tutorial-style, `usesScreens`)**: replaces the mode explanation, A/B intro, count tutorial and Text C/D/E below (kept in code, unused). Screen `slowing1` ("Part 1: Recording your pace", Continue) → reveal, counting on, `recordingEnabledRef` on, step text `MODE_SCREENS.slowingRecord` ("Breathe at your own pace.", with the Tutorial's 5 s "Remember to…" reminder, `tutorialRef.part 'record'`). When `SlowingDownController` finishes recording (`onGatesReady` → `handleRecordingReady`), `phase2StartRef = Infinity` holds the ramp and `tutorialGoBlank('slowing2')` covers the scene. Screen `slowing2` ("Part 2: Slowing down", Continue) → reveal + `runPreparedStart(false, …)` (prepare, 3, 2, 1), then `startPacedArt()` as "1" ends, with "Inhale" on the countdown line (fades in with the first bell, out after `COUNTDOWN_START_MS` 2 s): paced art on an Inhale at the recorded pace, the 60 s ramp starts. No Inhale/Exhale captions (`pacedCueStageRef 'done'`) and no idle re-show; bells and lit labels carry the pace.
- Display label: **"Guided Breathing: Slowing Down"** (internal mode id remains `'slowing'` — only the display string changed, in `MODE_LABELS.slowing`).
- **Text C** fires automatically after Text B (queued via `pendingGatesFnRef` thunk in `App.jsx`). Copy: "Breathe at your own pace for a few breaths." No auto-dismiss timer — stays visible until Initial Pace is set.
- **Recording phases** (managed by `SlowingDownController.jsx`):
  - `idle` — returns early until `recordingEnabledRef.current` is true (set by `showSlowingTextC` in App.jsx when Text C appears)
  - `warmup` — counts 1 full breath cycle (min→max→min, each ≥ `MIN_BREATH_SECONDS = 1.5`); not recorded
  - `recording` — records the next 2 cycles (3 breaths in all), averages them → **Initial Pace** (`avgBreathRef`); calls `onGatesReady()` callback
  - `gates` — ramps toward a fixed **Target Pace** over 60 seconds (`RAMP_SECONDS`) instead of "2× Initial Pace" — see Target Pace below; counts post-gate cycles for Text D/E
- **Target Pace** (replaces the old "ramp to 2× recorded pace" design): every user starts at their own recorded Initial Pace and ends at the same shared target, chosen up front via the **Breath Pace Options** screen (see Personalization system section) — persisted to localStorage as `targetPace` (`'6-6'` default, `'5-5'` or `'4-6'`, defined in `src/breathPace.js`'s `TARGET_PACES`). A small unlabeled **Change Target Pace** button (top-left 28×28 square matching Select Mode's Slider Layouts button, `mode === 'slowing'` only) reaches this screen mid-session; its Home button returns to `'experience'` (not `'selectMode'`, since it's reached mid-session — a deliberate deviation from every other screen's Home behavior).
  - `App.jsx` lifts two refs, `inhaleSecondsRef`/`exhaleSecondsRef`, initialized to `null` and reset to `null` unconditionally in `handleSelectMode` for every mode (plus again in `resetSlowingState`) — `null` is the sentinel every consumer (`Gates*.jsx`, `BackgroundA`/`BackgroundB`) treats as "no split, use the symmetric 1.5 ratio default." This prevents a stale asymmetric split from leaking into a shared `GatesComponent` instance if the user switches from a mid-ramp Slowing Down session into Paced Breathing.
  - `SlowingDownController.jsx`'s `gates`-phase block (replacing the old "×(1+t)" ramp) computes, every frame: `t = clamp((now - phase2StartRef)/RAMP_SECONDS, 0, 1)`, `startHalf = avgBreathRef/2`, then linearly interpolates `inhaleSecondsRef.current` from `startHalf` → `targetInhaleSeconds` and `exhaleSecondsRef.current` from `startHalf` → `targetExhaleSeconds`, and sets `spawnIntervalRef.current = inhaleSecondsRef.current + exhaleSecondsRef.current`. No "ramp done" guard is needed — this is idempotent once `t=1`, so it runs unconditionally for the rest of the session.
  - For the **5-5** target, `inhaleSecondsRef` and `exhaleSecondsRef` stay identical at every `t` (same start, same target) — the derived gate-spawn ratio stays exactly 1.5 throughout, so this option requires zero change to any Gate/Background file's ratio math, only the overall period (`spawnIntervalRef`) ramps toward a fixed 10 instead of `avgBreath × 2`.
  - For the **4-6** target, the split genuinely diverges, and each `Gates*.jsx`/`BackgroundA`/`BackgroundB` file uses the live `inhaleSecondsRef`/`exhaleSecondsRef` values to compute an asymmetric spawn-distance ratio (see the Gate geometry sections above and Background section below) — the exhale phase becomes visibly longer than the inhale phase as the ramp progresses.
  - Changing the target pace mid-ramp lets the interpolation redirect toward the new target for the remaining ramp time (or snap immediately if the ramp already finished) — not special-cased, just the natural consequence of the architecture.
- **D/E hand-off (Shapes D, E and B)**: Shape B (Morphing Cube) uses this same flow with its own copy (`TEXTS.slowingTextDCube` "Gates will soon move in at the rate you recorded. Breathe so the art fits through the gates." / `TEXTS.slowingTextECube` "Keep your breathing in sync with the gates as they move farther apart.", also the idle re-show text afterwards); `GatesB` gets the same `startOnInhale` (on enable: phase 'inhale', plus an extra Inhale target placed to arrive `inhaleSeconds` later, and the paced progress ramp started) and writes `breathPhaseRef` as the phase to breathe **next** (an Exhale target passing → 'inhale', an Inhale target passing → 'exhale') for `PacedPhaseWatcher`'s captions; it still writes `ringPaceProgressRef`, though the count now stays on the slider. recording done does NOT start the art. `handleSlowingRecordingDone` sets `phase2StartRef = Infinity` (ramp holds at the recorded pace), fades Text C, then `showPacedText('D')` shows Text D ("You will soon see art animate at the rate you recorded."), followed by Text E ("Keep your breathing in sync with the art as it slows down."). Each is timed exactly like the Diagonal intro's Text B2: 1 s fade-in (`DIAG_FADE_IN_MS`), hold for one left-slider breath cycle (`updatePacedText`, called from `setLeft`: `DIAG_CYCLE_STROKES` reversals with `DIAG_DEADBAND`, stage in `pacedTextStageRef`), 2 s fade-out (`DIAG_FADE_OUT_MS`). The controller's `onTextDone`/`onTextEDone` are no-ops for D/E. After Text E fades, `pacedWaitForBottomRef` arms; `setLeft` calls `startPacedArt()` once the left slider is at the bottom (`<= DIAG_EDGE_THRESHOLD`), which starts the ramp clock, starts count-ring counting, and enables gates. `GatesHeadless`/`GatesHeadlessE` get `startOnInhale` (true in Slowing Down): on enable they write `breathPhaseRef='inhale'` immediately and spawn an extra exhale checkpoint at `SPAWN_Z * inhale/P` so the first Exhale lands `inhaleSeconds` later. `PacedPhaseWatcher` reports each phase change to `handlePacedPhase`, which shows Box-style "Inhale"/"Exhale" captions for the first 5 breaths (`TutorialText` `pulseMode='paced'` + `pacedCaptionRef`: whole-phase fade × `PACED_CAPTION_ALPHA` [1,1,1,0.5,0.15]); nothing follows the captions. From Text E on, idle re-show falls back to Text E. The phase-locking below applies to Shapes A/C only.
- **Gate phase-locking**: when recording ends, gates are NOT enabled immediately. A delay `d` is computed so the first gate arrives at the Morph on the user's next inhale peak:
  - `lastMaxTimeRef` (ref passed from App.jsx) stores the exact timestamp of the last confirmed inhale peak
  - Single-gate shapes (C, D, E — GatesHeadless/GatesHeadlessE mirror GatesC's timing, spawn z=−20, travel time = 1 interval): `d = P − elapsed_since_max`
  - Dual-gate shapes (A/B, Gate B spawn z=−30, travel time = 1.5 intervals): `d ≈ 0` (Gate B naturally aligns)
  - `gateEnableTimerRef` fires after `d` seconds to set `gatesEnabledRef.current = true`
  - `phase2StartRef` is set to gate-enable time so the 60s ramp clock starts from the first visible gate
  - This math's fixed `1.5 * P` `T_travel` assumption (`App.jsx`) relies on `inhaleSecondsRef`/`exhaleSecondsRef` still being exactly `avgBreathRef/2` each (ratio 1.5) at `t=0` of the ramp — true regardless of the selected target pace, since the ramp always starts from that symmetric point.
- **Text D** fades in 2 seconds after Text C fades. Copy: "Good Job! The oncoming targets will begin at your pace and slow down over the next minute." No auto-dismiss — stays until user completes `TEXT_D_CYCLES = 3` post-gate breath cycles, then fades. `SlowingDownController` calls `onTextDone()` callback.
- **Text E** fades in 2 seconds after Text D fades. Copy: "Keep Morph aligned with the targets to slow down your breathing." No auto-dismiss — stays until user completes `TEXT_E_CYCLES = 4` more post-gate breath cycles, then fades. `SlowingDownController` calls `onTextEDone()` callback.
- **No post-ramp text or UI**: unlike the old design, nothing further appears once the ramp reaches its target — no Text F/G, no `BreathLengthControl`. The session simply continues at the target pace (or whatever the user last selected via Breath Pace Options) with the mode-label caption and the "Change Target Pace" button as the only persistent UI.
- **Stretched target**: if the starting pace P ≥ target total − `STRETCH_WITHIN_SECONDS` (2 s), i.e. slower, equal, or up to 2 s faster, the ramp's target becomes `STRETCH_FACTOR` (1.5) × P. It keeps the selected option's inhale/exhale proportion. This is recomputed every frame, so a mid-session Breath Pace Options change re-applies the rule.
- **Starting pace** = recorded average × `SLACK_FACTOR` (1.15) + `START_EXTRA_SECONDS` (1 s, split evenly over Inhale/Exhale). This is a demo tweak because the guided start felt faster than the user's real pace.
- Constants in `SlowingDownController.jsx`: `DEADBAND=0.08`, `MIN_BREATH_SECONDS=1.5`, `SLACK_FACTOR=1.15`, `WARMUP_CYCLES=1`, `RECORD_CYCLES=2`, `TEXT_D_CYCLES=3`, `TEXT_E_CYCLES=4`, `RAMP_SECONDS=60`
