## Gate geometry (Shape Option B — GatesB.jsx)
- **Exhale Gate (A)**: two RoundedBox bars at X=0, args [2.8, 0.25, 0.5] — one above the morph (Y=0.65), one below (Y=-0.15)
- **Inhale Gate (B)**: two RoundedBox pillars at Y=0.25, args [0.4, 4.5, 0.5] — one left (X=-0.9), one right (X=0.9)
- Pool: 3 slots each for Gate A and Gate B
- Ties: width sized to the Inhale pillars' inner-facing edges (`GATE_B_X - CUBE_ARGS[0]/2` = 0.65) — the Exhale bars span the full X width so they don't constrain it
- **Dynamic Inhale Gate (B) spawn z**: same `computeGateBZ()` mechanism as Option A (identical helper, same `inhaleSecondsRef`/`exhaleSecondsRef` props) — `GATE_B_X`/`GATE_B_Y` (the pillars' X/Y offsets) are unrelated and unaffected; only the pillar group's Z spawn position moves. Shape B's copy uses `1 + inhale/P` (not A's `1 + exhale/P`), so the gap from an Exhale target to the next Inhale target is the inhale time, as it should be on an asymmetric pace like 4-6.
- **Exhale look (Shape B only, `cubeMaterialB.js`)**: the target cubes use the cube Morph's own material (`createCubeMorphMaterial`, shared with MorphB, one instance per cube). From spawn until the pulse they wear the Morph's Exhale settings (tertiary base, opacity 0.5), but fully glossy (`GATE_APPROACH_ROUGHNESS` 0), with the Fresnel mask off and an unmasked primary glow at `GATE_APPROACH_EMISSIVE` 0.5. Over the pulse window (z −0.5 → 0, smoothstepped, `gateLookT`) they blend to their own pulse look (secondary base, opaque, unmasked emissive; roughness 0 in both, metalness 0.1 in `GatesB` and 0 in Box Breathing), which they keep past z=0. `GATE_EXHALE_LOOK = false` restores the plain target look throughout.
- **Emissive + visibility (Shape B only, `GatesB.jsx` and `GatesBoxBreathingB.jsx`)**: instead of the shared ramp below, each target's emissive ramps 0→1 (smoothstepped) from the moment it appears (z=-20 in `GatesB`; each target's own spawn z in Box Breathing) to z=-0.5, then pulses 1→2 at z=0 and holds. No fade-out at z=0–2: targets stay fully opaque until `DESPAWN_Z` (6), which is past the bottom of the screen for Shape B's camera.
- **Target reactions (`gateReactionsB.jsx`, Shape B only, also used by `GatesBoxBreathingB.jsx`)**: each target is judged once as it reaches z=0, against the raw right slider only (`rightVal`, passed from App.jsx; other skins' gate components ignore it). Inhale target succeeds at rightVal ≤ 0.05, Exhale at ≥ 0.95 (`SUCCESS_THRESHOLD` 0.95). **Success**: 150 burst particles (same look as the Morph's bursts: size 60, 1–2 s life, primary color) spawn on the surfaces of the target's two cubes at the crossing z and fly along X only, speed = random [0.15, 0.5] × x / the target's outermost |x| (Inhale 1.15, Exhale 0.25), so they move away from the Morph's axis, slowest at the center. **Miss**: over `MISS_RAMP_SECONDS` 0.25 the target's emissive ramps to 0 and each cube shrinks in place to `MISS_SCALE` 0.25 in X/Y. In Box Breathing every target in each series is judged individually.


## Breath count (Shape B, Morphing Cube — `breathCountB.jsx`)
Cube-only 5-breath count, driven by MorphB via `useBreathCountB`. It starts at the same per-mode points as MorphC (`breathCountingEnabledRef`) and, like MorphC, counts from the source in `breathCountSourceRef` (App.jsx selects it for `'b'` as it does for `'d'`):
- **Every mode (Own Pace, Box Breathing, Slowing Down):** the right slider (`1 − rightVal`, so 0 exhale → 1 inhale). The count never switches to a paced source now, so every Inhale the user takes adds a piece, whether or not it matches the pace. The paced-source path (`boxProgressRef` / `ringPaceProgressRef`, described below) is kept in code but unused:
  - *(unused)* **Box Breathing:** `boxProgressRef`, written by `GatesBoxBreathingB`. Each target series' approach is a movement phase (Inhale 0→1, Exhale 1→0, over one interval from spawn to its first target reaching the Morph), and its crossing is the following Hold. That gives one piece per box cycle.
  - *(unused)* **Slowing Down:** counting starts in `startPacedArt` (the shared D/E/B hand-off), from `ringPaceProgressRef`, written by `GatesB` (App passes it as `paceProgressRef`; `BackgroundRingsD`, its Shape D writer, isn't mounted for B). It's 1 as each Inhale target passes and 0 as each Exhale target passes, ramping between them over the next opposite target's exact arrival time.
- **Start:** the first piece is armed only if the source starts at or below `GROW_START`; otherwise it waits for the next trough, so Slowing Down (which starts counting as an Inhale target passes) doesn't pop piece 1 in fully grown.
- **Placement:** pieces are children of the cube's scaled group inside a counter-scale of 1/`INHALE_SCALE` [1.2, 3.5, 1.2], so they're true size at full Inhale and squash/stretch with the cube. Layouts are made in world units at Inhale and must fit inside the rounded cube (`insideMorph`).
- **Appear:** the next piece grows 0→1 (smoothstepped) as the slider goes `GROW_START` 0.25 → `LOCK_AT` 0.98, following the slider back down until it locks. Same deadband tracker as MorphC. **Slider-driven count** (no paced source — Own Pace and the tutorial cycles): no failed state. The confirmed start of each Inhale (8% deadband rise) counts the next piece at once, and it grows in on its own timer over `USER_APPEAR_S` 1 s (smoothstepped). Fast breaths overlap, and a shrink that starts mid-grow scales down from wherever it is.
- **Disappear:** once all 5 are locked, the downward turn that starts breath 5's Exhale shrinks them (`SHRINK_S` 1 s each, `SHRINK_STAGGER_S` 0.25 s apart; spheres in random order, stack top-down). Counting moves to the next set, and the palette change fires on breath 1 of the next group (`onBreathPaletteCycle`), like MorphC.
- **Sets** (7, in rotation order; each is one 5-breath group). **Towers** (`layoutTower`): 5 pieces on the Y axis, middle at the center, spaced as far apart as the end pieces allow (pieces may overlap), in random appearance order.
  - 0 = **sphere tower**: radius 0.5 (max), random X/Z offset that still fits (`sphereFits`); each pulses on an eased loop to 90% and back with its own random loop length (`PULSE_PERIOD_S` 1–1.5 s) and starting offset (`PULSE_DEPTH` 0.1).
  - 1 = **cube stack**: MorphC's tilted stack rules fitted to the cube (≈0.5 cubes, middle three widened to ≈0.74, random Y angle, Y spin from appearance).
  - 2 = **nested ovoids**: centered; #5 = the Inhale half-extents × `OVOID_FIT` 0.98, sizes `NEST_STEPS` 25% → 100%, appear smallest → largest.
  - 3 = **nested rounded cubes**: same steps; #5 = `NEST_CUBE_SIZE` 1.0 × 3.3 × 1.0 with the Morph's own corner rounding, a 0.1 gap from the Morph surface everywhere (checked numerically).
  - 4 = **tetrahedron tower**: MorphC's chamfered geometry at the largest circumradius that fits at the center (`MAX_CENTER_RADIUS` ≈ 0.59, so free spin never clips), no offset, random angles, spin on all axes.
  - 5 = **nested rings**: Pulse/Hold ring proportions (tube 0.045 on radius 1, `RING_TUBE`); set 2's nested sizes with #5's outer edge on the #5 ovoid, random appearance order, random Y angle, Y spin.
  - 6 = **circular-ring tower**: outer diameter 1.0 (the tower spheres'), same tower layout and X/Z offsets as set 0, Pulse/Hold proportions, random angles, spin on all axes.
  - Spins use `STACK_SPIN_SPEED` per axis. Shrink order: top-down (stack), largest first (nested ovoids/cubes), random otherwise.
- **Material:** MorphC's Count Cube look: **secondary** color/emissive, emissive 4, alpha 0.5, additive, no depth test. Alpha is scaled by `REF_LUMINANCE` 0.035 ÷ the live secondary's luminance (capped at 1): dark-secondary palettes are unchanged, and Light Blue's lighter secondary (#526539; Light Blue is currently out of the breath-cycle rotation, `BREATH_CYCLE_PALETTES` = Teal, Burnt Sienna, Pink) is dimmed to match instead of reading as a solid, overbright block.
- **Palette cycle:** the whole cube skin follows `livePaletteRef`: Morph (tertiary base, primary emissive), surface sparkles and count pieces (secondary), targets (secondary base, primary emissive), ties and target bursts (primary).

## Landscape (Shape B, Morphing Cube — `LandscapeB.jsx`)
Scenery shapes on both sides of the road that flow toward the camera with the targets; mounted by App.jsx only when `shapeOption === 'b'`, in every mode.
- **Kind** changes with each 5-breath group at the palette change: `landscapeIndexRef` (App.jsx, reset in `handleSelectMode`) is advanced by `breathCountB` when it fires `onBreathPaletteCycle` and reset when counting starts/stops. Order: spheres, rounded cubes, tetrahedrons, repeating. The kind is read at each spawn, so shapes already on screen keep theirs and the new landscape rolls in from the far end.
- **Spawn:** z = −20, y = 0, diameter random 1–3; each side independently every random 2–5 units of travel; |x| from `EXCLUDE_X` (1.5) + radius out to `X_SPREAD` (1) further, so they hug the road. Cubes get a random Y angle, tetrahedrons random angles on all axes; no spin. 1 s fade-in, removed past z = 7.5. The stretch to the camera is pre-filled at mount.
- **Speed:** Box Breathing `6 / spawnIntervalRef` (its targets' speed, though landscape spawns far at −20), Slowing Down/Paced `20 / spawnIntervalRef` (as `GatesB` and its ties), Breathe at Your Own Pace a fixed `20 / 12` drift.
- **Look:** the targets' approach look (`createCubeMorphMaterial` + `applyGateLook` at t=0: roughness 0.3, 50% see-through) with `LANDSCAPE_LOOK`: roughness 0.3, tertiary color for both base and emissive, emissive 0.5 (unmasked).
