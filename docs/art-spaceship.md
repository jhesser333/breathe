## Shape Option J — Space Ship (in-progress, `MorphJ.jsx`, `SpaceshipBayJ.jsx`, `spaceshipJ.js`)
Art Options card "Space Ship (in-progress)" (id `'j'`, after the Balloon), built for **Blow on the Mic**. The app paces a 5 s inhale; the user's exhale flies the ship toward the destination sphere, and about 5 s of exhale docks it. Docking ends the exhale, so going longer gains nothing. After docking, everything out there is pulled back to the bay while the user inhales, so it reads as the bay moving up to the ship. The ship reaches out, then the bay catches up, like an inchworm. The bay itself never moves.
- **Gating:** the card is grayed (no tap) unless the control is Blow on the Mic, with "Select Blow on the Mic in Control Options to use this art." While `'j'` is selected, Control Options blocks every other control (note: "Space Ship only works with Blow on the Mic…") and Home grays Box Breathing and Slowing Down ("Not available with Space Ship yet."). Selecting it sets the mode to Tutorial. On load, a saved `'j'` without `'blow'` falls back to `'d'`, and a saved `'j'` forces the mode to Tutorial.
- **Tutorial:** opens straight on Part 3 (Test Bell); Continue runs the usual paced start (prepare, 3-2-1), but at 5-5 (`startTutorialPaced` picks `TARGET_PACES['5-5']` for `'j'`), so the inhale is 5 s. No Part 1/2 (no 5-breath count). In `isAmbientArt`.
- **Camera:** the Morphing Cube's (`CAMERA_BY_SHAPE.j` `[0, 3.5, 5]`, fov 50, look-at-origin), with a lens shift: the bay works out its layout on an unshifted copy of the camera, then `setViewOffset` (pure shift, no zoom) drops the scene so the floor's front edge sits `FLOOR_SCREEN_PX_MIC` 96 px above the screen's bottom, clearing the stacked labels (on the Double Sliders fallback `FLOOR_SCREEN_PX` 80, just above Home/Restart; cleared on unmount).
- **Sliders hidden:** with Blow on the Mic, `SlidersDiagonal`'s `labelsOnly` hides the tracks and thumbs. Instead of the two "inhale" labels and the bottom "exhale", one "inhale" sits over "exhale" (`HOLD_LABEL_GAP` 4 px), between Home and Restart on their bottom line (`STACK_EXHALE_BOTTOM` 16); they still light up with the phase. On the Double Sliders fallback (mic failed) the sliders show.
- **Tilt flat to blow** (against false positives: noises the mic took for exhales): hold the phone upright to inhale and tilt it flat to exhale. The mic's blow only counts while the phone is within `FLAT_ON_DEG` 35° of flat (|beta|), and stops counting once it's tipped back up past `FLAT_OFF_DEG` 45° (`useBreathControl`'s `blowNeedsFlat`, which App sets for `'j'`; it also turns the tilt listener on). Tilting flat alone does nothing; the exhale still flies the ship, and tipping back up mid-flight is like the exhale stopping. No tilt reading (no sensor or motion access denied) leaves the gate open. The detector keeps running all the time (so the floor stays current, and a blow already going is heard the moment the phone tips flat); only its outputs are gated (`blowRef`, and `blowDetailRef`'s `candidate`/`early`/`strict`; `listening` says whether the gate is open). Side-to-side steering (roll, gamma) works with the phone flat. The mic check doesn't need the tilt. `?micdebug` shows `flat yes/no (beta N°)`, `?shipdebug` `flat yes/no`. (This replaced a hold-the-screen gate and its hold circle.)
- **Ship (`MorphJ`):** the Paper Airplane's pyramid (`WINGSPAN` 1.4, `THICKNESS` 0.25, `LENGTH` 2, tertiary base, primary emissive 0.3) plus 3 engine cylinders across the back (`ENGINE_X` −0.4/0/0.4, `ENGINE_R` 0.09, `ENGINE_LEN` 0.25). Transparent so it can fade in. Writes the live phone roll to `shipStateRef.gamma` (null = no reading); `exitDir` makes it accelerate sideways (`EXIT_SIDE_ACCEL` 2.5 u/s², ramping in over `EXIT_TURN_S` 1.5 s) off that side, nose turned along its velocity (yaw toward the side, pitched down with the dive). Draws from `shipStateRef` (written by the bay): `rev` → engine emissive 0 → `ENGINE_EMISSIVE_MAX` 3; `emit` → engine particles (world-space Points, `EMIT_RATE` 150/s, drifting back toward the camera, primary additive); `rock` → random roll swings up to `ROCK_MAX` 0.12 rad; `steer` → side tilt (gamma, `SIDE_TILT_FULL_DEG` 25° = ±`ship.sideRange`, written by the bay each frame: the view's half-width at the ship's depth (`halfWidthAt`) − `SHIP_EDGE_INSET` (0.3 × wingspan), so full tilt always puts the ship at the screen edge, a little of it clipped, and the reach widens as it flies away: ~0.9 at the bay → ~4.7 at the spheres on a 390×844 phone) with banking, only while flying; `xOverride` (number, null = none) pins x while arriving/docking. Writes `planePosRef` (x, y, z).
- **Heights (`shipLimits`):** `hoverY` = the airplane's `bottomY` (clear of the sliders); floor top `HOVER_GAP` 0.15 under the ship; `fullY` = `FULL_HEIGHT_FRAC` 0.5 of the way from the floor to the midpoint of the balloon's `restY` and `flightY` (that midpoint clamped between hoverY + 0.3 and the airplane's `topY`; `fullY` at least hoverY + 0.2); `columnX` near the screen's side edges at z = 0; `columnTop` = `COLUMN_HEIGHT_FRAC` 0.75 of the way from the floor to just past the screen's top.
- **Bay (`SpaceshipBayJ`):**
  - Floor: two door panels the size of the balloon's short barrier (3 × 0.2 × 1.6), `DOOR_GAP` 0.02 apart at x = 0, no emissive. They slide one panel width apart (`DOOR_SLIDE_S` 0.6) while the ship rises, and close under it.
  - Columns: tertiary, no emissive, at ±`columnX` (`COLUMN_R` 0.12). Glowing pair ×1.15 wider, emissive tertiary ×3, grows up from the floor over the pace's inhale so it covers the plain pair at the Exhale bell; stays up through the exhale and sinks (`COLUMN_SINK_S` 0.6) as the ship fades.
  - Front door: the balloon door look (live primary, `GATE_EMISSIVE`), at `FRONT_DOOR_Z` −1.4, top `FRONT_DOOR_CLEAR` 0.3 above the ship at full height. Sinks into the floor at the Exhale bell (`FRONT_DOOR_S` 0.5), rises with the next ship.
  - Cube targets: the Morphing Cube's target look (`createCubeMorphMaterial` + `applyGateLook`, pulse window measured from the ship) but fully opaque (opacity = the course fade only), edge `TARGET_SIZE` 0.5, at `fullY`, `CUBE_SPACING` 2 apart center to center, so three cube lengths of space between them (`makeCourse` in `spaceshipJ.js`; the cube count is fixed, `COURSE_SIZE` 27):
    - A straight middle line at x = 0 from z `FORK_Z` −3 to −19 (1 unit before the spheres' front edge), leading to the middle sphere.
    - A path to each side sphere, cubes at z −5 … −21: it starts beside the middle line's second cube (x = ±`BRANCH_START_X` 1, one cube's gap) heading straight ahead, and curves out in a simple circular arc through the side sphere's center (the circle tangent to straight ahead at the start), so it meets the sphere a little toward the middle from its nearest point (~16° on a 390×844 phone, where it reaches x ±3.1 at z −21). Worked out every frame by `resolveCourse` (from the unshifted camera), since the side spheres' x follows the screen. Path cubes inside or touching the sphere are hidden and never judged.
    - Following a path is optional (docking only needs the ship to reach a sphere); later it will give points.
    - Each cube is turned (Y) to follow its line.
    - The ship passing a cube judges it: hit (|x| within half wingspan + half cube) → pulse to ×2 + the cube burst (`useGateBurstB`); miss → glow to 0, shrink to 0.25.
    - Cubes at the same z share a `row`. As the ship passes a row, only its cube nearest the ship's x is judged; the others are skipped, not judged, and keep their approach look.
    - On a miss/exit, the old cubes fade out with the ship and a new course fades in (`CUBE_FADE_S` 1).
  - Destination spheres: one per `SPHERE_SIDES` entry (`[-1, 0, 1]`: left, middle, right) at `fullY`, the side ones almost touching their side of the screen: x = side × (`halfWidthAt(fullY, SPHERE_Z)` − `SPHERE_R` − `SPHERE_EDGE_MARGIN` 0.3), from `resolveCourse` each frame (±3.6 on a 390×844 phone), center z `SPHERE_Z` −22.75, radius `SPHERE_R` 1.5. Their material is `MeshStandardMaterial` with `side: BackSide` (inverted normals), so the near half is invisible and you see inside the far half. It's transparent, with no depth write and emissive `SPHERE_EMISSIVE` 0.4.
    - **Worlds:** each sphere is a world index k: `BREATH_CYCLE_PALETTES[k]` (the sphere's tertiary base and primary glow) plus slider audio group k.
    - A new course (`makeCourse(currentWorld)`, with `currentWorldRef` = App's `paletteCycleIndexRef`) gives each sphere a world that isn't current. One sphere: the next world, so each dock moves on through the palettes and loops. Several: the other worlds, then (with three spheres and three palettes) the current one too, in random order, so one sphere keeps the current world.
    - Pool of 2 × `SPHERE_COUNT` meshes: the first `SPHERE_COUNT` are the current course's spheres, the rest the ones being docked.
- **Phases** (`shipPaceRef.phase`):
  - `idle`: before `gatesEnabledRef`; the ship waits under the floor.
  - `inhale` (Inhale bell, after `idle` or an exit):
    - The ship fades in and rises over `RISE_DELAY_S` 0.3 + `RISE_S` 1.2 to hoverY, then floats to fullY by the end of the inhale.
    - Rev/rock/emit and the glow columns follow inhale progress. Blowing is ignored.
  - `ready` (Exhale bell): the front door sinks, and the ship waits for the first blow with no time limit.
  - `flying`:
    - Steady `SHIP_SPEED` 5 u/s whatever the blow strength, with Y fixed.
    - `STOP_GRACE_S` 0.5 without a blow → `slowing` (rides out brief mic dropouts).
  - `slowing`: × `SLOW_FACTOR` ¼ speed for `SLOW_S` 1.5 s; a blow → `flying`.
  - `arriving` (from `flying`/`slowing`):
    - Triggers when the ship enters a sphere: seen from above, its center within `SPHERE_R` 1.5 of a sphere's center (the nearest one if several), from the front or a little from the side as the curved side paths arrive. Past the spheres' centers without entering one is a miss. There's no collision; this check alone decides.
    - The ship eases out to a stop at that sphere's center (z ease-out whose starting speed matches its current speed: `ARRIVE_S` 0.6 s at full speed). Its x eases (smoothstep) onto the sphere's x via `xOverride`, over at least |dx| / `ARRIVE_SIDE_SPEED` 3 s; docking starts when both eases finish. Blowing is ignored.
    - Timing: the mic needs `START_LAG_S` 0.15 s to be sure it's a blow (the early detector's window, `BLOW_EARLY_S`), then the front edge comes after 4.25 s of full-speed flying + 0.6 s ease = a 5 s exhale (`TARGET_EXHALE_S`) from its real start.
    - Otherwise (off to the side), the ship flies on to `COURSE_END_Z` (= `SPHERE_Z`), dives and respawns under the dock.
  - `docking` (the next inhale, Inhale bell; App maps it to 'inhale'):
    - **Pull:** over the inhale time, `k = smoothstep(t)`. Ship, both spheres and the old cubes all shift by (−fromX·k, 0, −fromZ·k), so the ship goes from the sphere's center to (0, fullY, 0).
    - **Spheres:** the chosen one grows ×1 → ×`DOCK_GROW_SCALE` 4 by `DOCK_GROW_END` 70% and fades out from `DOCK_FADE_START` 40% to the end. The other is gone by `DOCK_OTHER_FADE_END` 20%, before the growing sphere could take it in.
    - **Cubes:** the old ones fade out by `DOCK_CUBE_FADE_END` 40%. At `DOCK_NEW_COURSE_AT` 75%, the new course (made at dock start from the chosen world) swaps in and fades in over `CUBE_FADE_S`.
    - **Audio and palette:** `onSphereAudio(world, inhaleDur)` fires at dock start; App sets `audioGroupIndexRef` and calls `setCycle(world, inhaleDur)`, a crossfade over the whole inhale. `onSpherePalette(world, 60% of inhaleDur, now)` fires at 40%; App's `startPaletteLerpTo` lerps the live palette over the chosen sphere's fade.
    - **Bay:** the glow columns sink (≤ `COLUMN_SINK_S`) and regrow over the rest of the inhale. The front door stays down. The floor doors open as the pull starts and slide closed over the last `DOOR_SLIDE_S` 0.6 s, so they meet under the ship as it reaches x = 0 (the bay seems to travel out to the ship).
    - Then `ready`.
  - `exiting`:
    - The side comes from the phone's roll, random under `EXIT_TILT_DEADZONE_DEG` 3° or with no reading.
    - Forward speed eases from ¼ speed to `EXIT_FORWARD_SPEED` 1.5 over 1 s, while the dive (`EXIT_DOWN_ACCEL` 2.5 u/s²) and the side pull ramp in over `EXIT_TURN_S`, so it curves gently off its line.
    - It fades out from `EXIT_FADE_START_S` 1.8 s to `EXIT_MAX_S` 2.8 s, or pops once it projects past `EXIT_OFFSCREEN_NDC` 1.15 off the screen. Cubes and spheres fade, columns sink.
    - Then a new course in the same world and the next `inhale` (a new ship rises from the floor). The palette and audio don't change.
  - `?shipdebug` in the URL shows a readout: phase, ship z (vs the spheres' front at −21.25), x and whether it's lined up with a sphere, seconds of blow this trip, the strict, candidate and early detectors, tone and the rule blocking the strict one, and how the last trip ended (docked / short / missed the sphere), plus a held `blow lost:` line: each time the mic stops hearing the blow mid-flight, how long into the blow, the off rule that turned the strict detector off (`blow.offWhy`) and the rise then, and the rule blocking the candidate.
  - Blowing on the mic = the strict detector (`blowRef`), the candidate or the early detector (`blowDetailRef`, written by `useBreathControl`; see Blow on the Mic). It's used for both the start (`ready` → `flying`) and keeping flying (`flying`/`slowing`). The early detector lets a gentle exhale start the ship within ~0.15 s and keep it going (the user used to blow harder to get a late start going, then ran short of breath).
    - The candidate has a looser tone limit, so the ringing Exhale bell (tonal) or a swell in the music can't hold back or interrupt a real blow.
    - It never uses the loose detector, which any sound passes, so taps, bumps and voices can't start or extend a flight.
    - **The bell is cut** as the ship leaves (`onFlightStart` → `stopBell`), as the mic check does once a blow starts.
    - Mic failed (Double Sliders fallback): the breath value falling faster than `SLIDER_BLOW_RATE` 0.08/s.
- **Coaching text** (`onShipText` → App's `handleShipText`, `fadeToText` / `hideCountText`, 1 s fades; copy `TEXTS.shipInhaleA/B`, `shipBlow`): each Inhale bell (rising or docking) rotates "Hold your phone upright and slowly fill your lungs." → "Exhale for about six seconds to reach a sphere." → "Tilt your phone to choose a sphere." (`shipInhaleA`, `shipInhaleB`, `shipChoose`), hidden `INHALE_TEXT_S` 4 s after fading in. While `ready`, "Tilt your phone flat and blow on the mic." shows `BLOW_PROMPT_S` 1 s after fading in, then comes back `BLOW_PROMPT_WAIT_S` 3 s after fading out, until the first blow. The Tutorial's closing "Good job!" text is skipped for `'j'`.
- **App wiring:** `balloonPhaseNow` also covers `'j'` (`inhale`/`docking` → 'inhale', `ready`/`flying`/`slowing`/`arriving`/`exiting` → 'exhale', `idle` → null), so bells, lit labels and `getQuietRate` (1 / inhale time) follow the ship. No captions or count; the Tutorial's closing text still shows. `shipStateRef`/`shipPaceRef` reset on Start/Restart; motion access asked in the card tap and Start/Restart.
