## Shape Option G — Paper Airplane (in-progress, `MorphG.jsx`)
Art Options card "Paper Airplane (in-progress)" (id `'g'`, after the balloon). Ported from the PaperPlane project's `PlaneMorph.jsx`: the same gray-box plane (4-sided pyramid, `WINGSPAN` 1.4, `THICKNESS` 0.25, `LENGTH` 2, tertiary base, primary emissive 0.3, flat shading), smooth-damp easing, bank/pitch from velocity and floating roll/bob (at every height; PaperPlane's calm-at-the-bottom fade is dropped). Camera `CAMERA_BY_SHAPE.g`: `[0, 7, 10]`, fov 50, no `rotation` key, so R3F's look-at-origin gives the Morphing Cube's ~35° downward angle. It sits twice as far back as the cube's `[0, 3.5, 5]`; at the cube's distance the view at the plane's depth is only ~2 units wide, so the plane nearly filled the width and the side gates couldn't be cleared.
- **Up/down (breath, `leftVal` = breathRef, smoothstepped):** target y = lerp(top, bottom, lv), from `planeLimits` (`planeCloudsG.js`, shared with the cloud gates). Works for any camera angle: `hitFlightPlane` casts the camera ray through a screen point onto the flight plane z = 0. Bottom (the low ring's center) = where the row `SLIDER_CLEAR_PX` 250 px above the screen's bottom edge meets it + `PLANE_HALF_H`, clearing the sliders. Top = the high ring's center, bottomY + `BAR_H` + `RING_GAP` (4.2), or where the screen's top edge meets it − `PLANE_HALF_H` 0.35 − `EDGE_MARGIN` 0.15 if that's lower. The target height uses `smoothstep(breath, ARRIVE_LOW 0.2, ARRIVE_HIGH 0.8)`, so it reaches full Inhale/Exhale height 80% of the way through the slider's travel. Side reach `spanX` = the narrower of the view's half-widths at those two rows − `PLANE_HALF_W` − `EDGE_MARGIN` (the near, lower row is narrower with the tilted camera). Eased with `RISE_TIME` 1.5 s going up (Exhale) and `SINK_TIME` 2.5 s going down, so Inhale drifts it down slowly.
- **Blow glow:** with Blow on the Mic, the plane's emissive doubles (0.3 → 0.6) while the mic hears a blow. `useBreathControl` writes `blowRef` (App: 1 while `blow.on`, else 0, reset on cleanup), and MorphG eases toward it over `BLOW_GLOW_SMOOTH_S` 0.15 s. Other controls leave it at 0.
- **Sideways (side tilt):** `deviceorientation` gamma mapped as position: `SIDE_TILT_FULL_DEG` 25° = the side edge, level = center, eased over `SIDE_TIME` 1.5 s. Motion access is asked for in the card tap and in Start/Restart (`requestTiltPermission`). No sensor/permission = stays centered.
- **Gates: bar + ring targets (`planeCloudsG.js` bar/ring section, `GatesG.jsx`, `GatesBoxBreathingG.jsx`).** The cloud gates are parked: the cloud layout functions (`makePlaneGate`, `hitsPlane`, `sampleGateSurface`, `applyPlaneGate`; `MAIN_SCALE` 0.55, `SIDE_SCALE` 0.25, `GATE_LINE_FRAC` 0.5) stay in `planeCloudsG.js` unused, and the full cloud version is commit `0a5b740`. The rounded-cube side walls with fall/tip-over are commit `a59f825`. Each gate (`makePlaneCubeGate` → `{ bar, ring }`) has one material per part per slot. **Bars:** `createPlaneGateMaterial` + `applyPlaneGateLook`: opaque (depth write on; 1 s fade-in), live text base, emissive `GATE_EMISSIVE` 0.2 in the live secondary, pulsing ×1 → ×2 over z −0.5 → 0. **Rings:** the Morphing Cube targets' look, exactly: `createCubeMorphMaterial(secondary, primary)` + `applyGateLook(gateLookT(z), fade, ringPulseEmissive(z) × (1 − miss))` (50% see-through tertiary with 0.5 primary glow on approach, blending to solid secondary with emissive 1 → 2 over z −0.5 → 0).
  - **Ring target, every gate:** `TorusGeometry` facing the camera, radius `RING_R` = the wingspan 1.4 (to the tube's center), tube radius `RING_TUBE` 0.08. x = side × `SIDE_CENTER_FRAC` 0.58 × spanX on a random side (≈ ±0.66 on a typical phone; one side per series in Box Breathing). Inhale rings are centered on bottomY and touch the ground plane. Exhale rings are centered on topY, their bottom edge `RING_GAP` 0.2 above the bar's top.
  - **Bar, Exhale gates only** (`BAR_W` = `CUBE_SIZE` 2 × `BAR_SCALE_X` 15 = 30 wide, `BAR_H` = `CUBE_SIZE` × `BAR_SCALE_Y` 2 = 4 tall, `RoundedBoxGeometry`, `CUBE_RADIUS` 0.2), its bottom resting on the ground plane, so the plane flies over it.
  - **Judging** at z = 0 against the plane's rendered position (`planePosRef`, written by MorphG), like the Morphing Cube's targets. **Ring:** `ringContainsPlane` (plane center within `RING_HIT_R` = `RING_R` − `RING_TUBE` − `PLANE_HALF_H` ≈ 0.97 of the ring's center) → the cube's burst (`useGateBurstB` default size 60), sampled on the torus (`sampleRingSurface`), X-only speed measured from the ring's center × `RING_MAX_ABS_X` / `CUBE_INHALE_MAX_ABS_X`, then the ring fades out over `RING_FADE_OUT_S` 1 s; otherwise the ring's glow ramps to 0 and it shrinks to `MISS_SCALE` 0.25 (`missFactor`, `applyMissScale`). **Bar:** `hitsPlaneBar` (box overlap, plane padded by `PLANE_HIT_PAD` 0.6, bar inset `CUBE_HIT_INSET` 0.1) dims the bar's glow; no burst, no shrink.
  - **After crossing:** bars and missed rings don't fall or fade. They keep scrolling toward the camera and disappears once its top-front point projects below the screen's bottom edge (`isOffScreenBelow`, `barTopFront`, `ringTopFront`); the slot frees when both are gone, or at `DESPAWN_Z` 10 as a fallback.
- **Ground ties (`GroundTiesG.jsx`, every mode):** thin rounded bars across X (`TIE_WIDTH` = `BAR_W` 30, `TIE_HEIGHT` 0.04, `TIE_DEPTH` 0.08), live primary color, opacity `TIE_ALPHA` 0.15, on the ground plane `groundY` = bottomY − (`RING_R` + `RING_TUBE`) (from `planeLimits` each frame; the bar and the low rings rest on it). Spaced `TIE_SPACING` 2 from `TIE_FAR_Z` −40 to `TIE_NEAR_Z` 10, scrolling toward the camera and wrapping, fading in over the farthest `TIE_FADE` 5. Opacity also fades linearly with distance: × 1 at `DEPTH_FADE_NEAR_Z` 0 (nearer stays × 1) → × 0 at `DEPTH_FADE_FAR_Z` −50. Speed follows the gates (`LandscapeB`'s rule, `landscapeMode`): Box `6 / spawnInterval`, Slowing Down/Paced `20 / spawnInterval`, Own Pace/Tutorial `20 / 12`. It's an even scroll, not anchored to gates. On a 390×844 phone the ties span the screen from ~44 px down past the sliders.
  - **Timing:** `GatesG` = `GatesF`'s (Slowing Down/Paced: Exhale gate at −20, Inhale gate at `computeGateBZ`, `startOnInhale`, `breathPhaseRef`, `paceProgressRef`; pool 12), but each cycle spawns one interval further back (`LOOKAHEAD_Z` 20, same speed, so arrival times don't change; on enable the first cycle spawns at the usual spots plus one lookahead cycle). Gates are hidden beyond `VISIBLE_Z` −30, so at each crossing the next two are already visible. `GatesBoxBreathingG` = `GatesBoxBreathingF`'s series (`BOX_COUNTS` per phase, `onFirstGate`/`onLastGate`, `boxProgressRef`; pool 28), but a last gate crossing spawns the series *after next* (same type) one series length further back (−18 … −24), and on enable both the Inhale series (−6) and the Exhale series after it spawn; `startSeriesClock` drives `boxProgressRef` for the series arriving next.
- **Flow:** no count or background yet. It's in `isAmbientArt` (App.jsx, with D and E) for ambient copy and the paced hand-off. No 5-breath count, so Tutorial Part 2 (waits for 10 counted breaths) doesn't advance with this art, and Own Pace's count captions don't show.
