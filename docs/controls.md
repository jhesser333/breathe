## Slider controls

**Right slider drives everything (for now).** The left slider stays on screen and works (`leftVal`, `leftRawRef` still update), and now drives only the slider audio pair (see Audio). App.jsx exposes the right slider in the left slider's old convention (0 exhale → 1 inhale) as `breathRef` (`1 − rightVal`) and `breathRawRef` (`1 − rightRawRef`, the right slider's unclamped raw ratio from `useTouchSlider`), and passes them wherever the left slider used to go: every Morph's `leftVal`/`leftRawRef` props, `SlowingDownController`'s breath recording, the Diagonal Text A1/A2/B1/B2 sequence (`updateDiagonalSequence`), the Slowing Down D/E hold text (`updatePacedText`), the "sliders at the bottom" start (`startPacedArt` fires when the right slider reaches its top, i.e. Exhale), and idle detection (`setLeft` no longer counts as movement). Where the sections below say "left slider", read the right slider via these refs.

**Convention: every value driven by a slider eases in and out by default.** Each Morph component reads `leftVal.current`/`rightVal.current` and immediately passes them through `THREE.MathUtils.smoothstep(v, 0, 1)` once at the top of `useFrame`, then uses those eased `lv`/`rv` for every derived lerp/scale/material property — rather than tracking the thumb's raw position 1:1. New slider-driven properties should build on the same eased `lv`/`rv`, not the raw ref values. (Detectors that need the raw, un-eased signal — e.g. `SlowingDownController`'s breath-cycle zigzag detection via `leftRawRef` — are an intentional exception, since they're reading input, not animating output.)

| Slider | Label (top/bottom) | Controls |
|--------|-------------------|----------|
| Left (0=bottom=exhale, 1=top=inhale) | inhale / exhale | X/Z scale + Fresnel inner glow intensity |
| Right (0=bottom=inhale, 1=top=exhale) | exhale / inhale | Y scale + emissive intensity |

The mappings below are for **Shape A** (`MorphA.jsx`). **Shape B** (`MorphB.jsx`, Morphing Cube) is driven by the **right slider only** — scale and material alike (the left slider is reserved for audio later): X lerp(1.2, 2.2, rv), Z lerp(1.2, 0.5, rv), Y lerp(3.5, 0.4, rv), so rv 0 = Inhale (tall, narrow), rv 1 = Exhale (wide, flat). Its material has the same parameters as the Morphing Sphere's (emissive, roughness, opacity, Fresnel power/intensity, dissolve) and is driven by the **right slider only**, via paired `INHALE_*`/`EXHALE_*` constants. Exhale is the default: emissive 1.5, roughness 0, opacity 0.5, Fresnel power 0, dissolve 0 (solid). At Inhale only Fresnel power changes, to 0.2; the other pairs are equal for now. **Even shading (`flatShade`, Morph only):** the material (`createCubeMorphMaterial` in `cubeMaterialB.js`) shades every point as if it faced the camera like the cube's front face (world +Z normal for lighting, specular and the Fresnel mask), so the top and the Y-stretched rounded edges match the front instead of going darker; targets and landscape shapes use the same material without it. **Burst sparkles** (currently switched off via `BURSTS_ENABLED = false`; code kept): the sphere's surface-sparkle look at half size (size 60, 1–2 s life, twinkle, primary color, additive; pool 1500), but `BURST_COUNT` (150) spawn at once on the cube's visible surface each time the raw right slider reaches its top or bottom (`BURST_EDGE` 0.02, re-arms after moving `BURST_REARM` 0.1 back). Top (Exhale): each flies outward along X; bottom (Inhale): along Y. Speed = random `BURST_SPEED` [0.15, 0.5] units/s × its unscaled distance from the pivot along that axis ÷ 0.5, so 0 at the center and full at the face. The points sit outside the scaled group so the travel isn't stretched. **Surface sparkles**: a second system with the same look but the **secondary** color and no velocity, emitted steadily at `SURFACE_SPAWN_RATE` 300/s (pool 1500) at fixed points on the unit cube's surface, inside the scaled group so they stick to the mesh. **Drifting sparkles**: a third system with the surface sparkles' look and rate (secondary, 300/s, pool 1500), spawned on a random face of the cube at its current scale, outside the scaled group so they don't stick, each drifting straight out along that face's normal at a random `DRIFT_SPEED` [0.03, 0.1] units/s. Shapes C/D (`MorphC.jsx`) use their own — see "Shapes C/D (Morphing Sphere)" after this list.

**Left slider (lv):**
- X scale: lerp(2.2, 1.2, lv) — wide at exhale, narrow at inhale
- Z scale: lerp(0.5, 1.2, lv)
- Fresnel intensity: constant 1.0
- Fresnel power: lerp(0.2, 1.5, lv) — exhale has a much narrower, more concentrated glow (almost fully dark except a small center spot); inhale uses the wider/brighter glow that was previously the exhale mask

**Right slider (rv):**
- Y scale: lerp(3.5, 0.4, rv) — tall at inhale, flat at exhale
- Emissive intensity: piecewise — 2 at inhale (rv=0), dips to 1 at rv=0.85 (15% of the way from exhale to inhale), rises to 3 at exhale (rv=1)
- Roughness: lerp(0.3, 1, rv) — smoother at inhale, rougher at exhale

**Shapes C/D (Morphing Sphere, `MorphC.jsx`)** — Shape D's values shown (the `OPTION_D_*` constants); Shape C uses the older shared scales noted in the code:
- Right slider, inhale half (`lv` = the eased right slider inverted, 1 − rv: 0 exhale → 1 inhale). These were on the left slider; every Morphing Sphere visual now follows the right slider, and the left one is reserved for audio later:
  - X scale: 3 → 2
  - Z scale: 0.25 → 2
  - Fresnel power: 0 → 0.2
  - Flow particles (system 2): full rate only mid-travel, tapering to 0 toward both ends (`smoothstep(1−lv, 0.75, 1)` / `smoothstep(lv, 0.5, 0.75)`), drifting in the slider's current direction
- Right slider (rv, inhale → exhale):
  - Y scale: 3 → 0.25
  - Emissive intensity: 1.5 → 0
  - Roughness: 0.3 → 1
  - Opacity: 0.75 → 0
  - Dissolve into grain across the full travel (solid at inhale, gone at exhale)
  - Surface sparkles (system 1): full rate until rv 0.75, then ramp to 0

- Left slider starts at 0 (bottom / Exhale). Right slider starts at 1 (top / Exhale).
- Morph starts in Exhale state: wide flat disc.
- Slider values are refs (not state) to avoid re-renders. Updates happen in `useFrame`.
- Slider fill indicator shows progress from exhale toward inhale on both sliders.
- The left slider also exposes a raw (unclamped) ratio via `leftRawRef`, tracking the thumb's true position even past the slider's visual bounds — used by `SlowingDownController` for breath-cycle timing in "Slowing Down" mode.
- **Slider Layout**: a persisted preference (`sliderLayout`, localStorage key `sliderLayout`, values `'vertical'` (hidden)/`'diagonal'`/`'single'`/`'touch'`/`'tilt'`/`'blow'`, default `'diagonal'`), chosen on the **Control Options** screen (formerly Slider Layouts; the new driven controls use the Diagonal look) (see Personalization system below). `'vertical'` renders `Sliders.jsx` (unchanged, described above); `'diagonal'` renders `SlidersDiagonal.jsx` — two curved "hook" tracks (an SVG circular-arc path per side) rendered near the bottom of the screen (`bottom: 16`, matching the Home/Restart nav), each a circular arc (even radius, `ARC_DEGREES` 60° of turn, radius ≈ 164px) rising near-vertically from a close-together exhale end at the bottom and bending outward (~62° at the top) to an inhale end inset from the screen edge (~136px from center); track 54px thick, thumb 60px; labels 22px bold in the live tertiary color (the phase to breathe now eases to the text color — see Color Palettes) — "inhale" centered above each thumb's top position (14px gap), "exhale" centered just below the tracks' bottom caps (6px gap); Home/Restart nav pinned to the bottom corners. Slider *value* semantics are identical regardless of layout (0/1 mean the same thing for `lv`/`rv` either way) — only the on-screen orientation changes. `useTouchSlider.js` takes a 3rd `orientation` param (`'vertical'` default, `'diagonal-left'`, `'diagonal-right'`) that changes how the ratio is computed from a drag point: `'vertical'` uses the single-axis `1 - (coord - start)/size` formula as before (on `clientY`/`rect.top`/`rect.height`); the two diagonal orientations normalize the raw `(clientX, clientY)` point into the track's local curve-box coordinates and call `projectToCurve` (from `diagonalCurveGeometry.js`) to find the nearest point on that side's arc, returning an arc-length fraction (0=exhale end, 1=inhale end) used as the ratio (mirrored for the right side). See `diagonalCurveGeometry.js` below for the curve math shared by rendering and hit-testing.


- **Control Options screen** (`SliderLayoutsScreen.jsx`, screen id `'sliderLayouts'`, title **CONTROL OPTIONS** styled like Art Options') — 5 selectable cards (same `optionBtn` ✓ pattern, persisted via `sliderLayout`): **Double Sliders** (`'diagonal'`, default — the Diagonal sliders), **Single Slider** (`'single'`), **Touch the Screen** (`'touch'`), **Tilt Your Phone** (`'tilt'`), **Blow on the Mic** (`'blow'`). The Vertical card is hidden (`'vertical'` still works in code; a saved `'vertical'` is moved to `'diagonal'` on load). Bottom row: see Option screens' bottom row below.
  - **Driven controls** (`useBreathControl.js`, called in App as `drivenBreath`): for single/touch/tilt/blow, a rAF loop holds the breath value v (0 exhale → 1 inhale) and, whenever it changes, writes `leftRawRef`/`rightRawRef` and calls `setLeft(v)`/`setRight(1 − v)` exactly as if both sliders moved, so everything downstream is unchanged. `SlidersDiagonal`'s `drivenValue` prop shows v on the thumbs and makes the tracks ignore touches. Input uses window pointer events (mouse works too), ignores pointers on buttons and everything while `slidersDisabledRef` is on (covered screens); v resets to 0 with `sliderResetKey`.
  - **Single Slider**: drag distance moves v (`SINGLE_TRAVEL_FRAC` 1/3 of the screen height = full travel, up = inhale; moves under `STILL_PX` 2 px ignored). Still for `STILL_MS` 120 ms, or lifted, partway: v glides on in its last direction at the finger's smoothed speed (never slower than `MIN_GLIDE_PER_S` 1/3) to the end and stays. A move the other way takes over at once.
  - **Touch the Screen**: any pointer down → v rises toward 1, none → falls toward 0, at `TOUCH_RATE_PER_S` 1/3 (full trip 3 s, fixed speed), holding at the ends.
  - **Tilt Your Phone**: `deviceorientation` beta mapped linearly `TILT_EXHALE_DEG` 25° (full Exhale) → `TILT_INHALE_DEG` 65° (full Inhale), low-passed over `TILT_SMOOTH_S` 0.15 s. No calibration step. `requestTiltPermission()` (iOS motion prompt) runs inside the Tilt card tap and every Start/Restart. Denied, no API, or no reading within `TILT_NO_READING_MS` 1 s → `tiltFailed`: the session falls back to touchable Double Sliders and the Tilt card shows "Motion access is off, so Double Sliders will be used."
  - **Blow on the Mic** (`'blow'`): mic only (the tilt + blow combo was removed). Blowing (`blow.on`) = exhale: v falls at `BLOW_MIN_RATE_PER_S` 1/6 → `BLOW_MAX_RATE_PER_S` 1/2 by blow strength. Quiet for `BLOW_RELEASE_MS` 300 ms = inhale: v rises at App's `getQuietRate()` (the pace's inhale speed once a pace runs — Box: 1/step; Slowing Down/Tutorial: 1/inhale time; Balloon: 1/its inhale time — else `BLOW_INHALE_RATE_PER_S` 1/4). A finger on the screen (not on a button) holds; blowing still exhales. With `blowNeedsFlat` (Space Ship) the blow only counts while the phone is tilted flat (see art-spaceship.md → Tilt flat to blow). The mic is opened by `requestMicAccess()` inside the Start/Restart tap (the card tap calls `requestMicAccess(false)`, which only asks permission and lets go), with echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto-gain off. It's analysed in its own `AudioContext` (analyser → muted gain → output, as Safari needs), separate from `breathAudio`'s. The detection itself is in `blowDetector.js` (pure, no React or DOM; `analyzeBlowFrame` each frame, `buildProfile` for the mic check), so it can be tested offline with synthetic spectra. Each frame, `BLOW_LOW_HZ` 50 – `BLOW_HIGH_HZ` 8000 Hz (from the low wind-rumble of a close blow up to the hiss of a breath from arm's length) is read at `FFT_SIZE` 2048 (~23 Hz bins). Each bin is smoothed over `BAND_SMOOTH_S` 0.1 s and compared with **its own noise floor**, so steady room noise (a fan, white noise) cancels out, and bins are summed into 12 roughly half-octave bands (`BAND_EDGES_HZ` 50, 120, 200, 300, 450, 650, 900, 1250, 1750, 2500, 3500, 5000, 8000). Each bin's floor is its lowest level over the last `FLOOR_WINDOW_S` 2 s (`FLOOR_SLOTS` 8 sub-windows, + `FLOOR_BIAS_DB` 1.5, smoothed `FLOOR_SMOOTH_S` 0.2 s), so it follows a quieter or louder room within about 2 s. It's held while the strict detector or the candidate hears a blow, and while the loose one does during the mic check's exhale step or within `FLOOR_HANGOVER_S` 2 s of a strict blow (so a flickering blow can't seep into it, but a stray noise alone doesn't freeze it), up to `FLOOR_FREEZE_MAX_S` 8 s (so a sudden louder room reads as a blow for at most that long). The first `BLOW_CALIBRATE_S` 0.5 s of audio fills the floor. Three detectors run:
    - **Loose** (`blow.raw`, used by the mic check to find blows): over `BLOW_ON_COVERAGE` 35% of bands `BAND_ON_DB` 3 dB above their floor and an average rise over `BLOW_ON_DB` 3 dB; off below `BLOW_OFF_COVERAGE` 20% or `BLOW_OFF_DB` 1.5 dB.
    - **Candidate** (`blow.candidate`), all of:
      - **Lasts:** over the last `BLOW_START_S` 0.3 s, at least `BLOW_SUSTAIN_FRAC` 80% of the frames have their own *unsmoothed* rise (`frameExcess`, the mean per-band dB over the floor) above `levelOn`. A tap, knock or bump fills about 3 frames, so it fails; judging the smoothed level instead would stretch a tap into ~0.4 s of "blow".
      - **Steady:** `jitter` (the mean frame-to-frame change of `frameExcess` in that window) at most `jitterMax` (no profile: `BLOW_MAX_JITTER_DB` 5). Speech jumps at syllables, rustling is jagged.
      - The smoothed average rise over `levelOn` (no profile: `BLOW_ON_DB` 3).
      - Coverage over `BLOW_MATCHED_COVERAGE` 20% (no profile: `BLOW_ON_COVERAGE` 35%).
      - With a profile, the **shape match** over `matchOn`: the Pearson correlation of the per-band rise with the profile's template (both mean-centred, so loudness drops out and only the shape counts; a rise with under `SHAPE_MIN_SPREAD_DB` 0.5 rms spread has match 0). A flat or high-heavy sound scores near 0 or below against a low-heavy blow.
      - **Tone** under the looser of `toneMax` and `BLOW_MAX_TONE` 0.75. Tone = how peaky the sound added over the floor is from bin to bin, 50–`TONE_HIGH_HZ` 3000 Hz (each bin vs the mean of its `TONE_SPAN` ±3 neighbours, Σ|dev| ÷ Σmean, smoothed `TONE_SMOOTH_S` 0.15 s): a breath is smooth (low), a voice's harmonics and the app's bells/music are peaky (high). The looser limit lets a blow through while the Exhale bell still rings.
    - **Strict** (`blow.on`, drives the control): turns on when the candidate holds and tone is under the profile's `toneMax` (no profile: `BLOW_MAX_TONE`). Off after `BLOW_RELEASE_S` 0.15 s below `levelOff`, match below `matchOff` (no profile or shapeless: coverage below `BLOW_OFF_COVERAGE`), or tone over `toneMax` × 1.2, so a tap during a blow changes nothing. `blow.why` names the first rule failing (level / sustain / steady / coverage / shape / tone; empty while on).
    - **Early** (`blow.early`, used by the Space Ship only): the strict detector's keep rules held over a short window, for the gentle start of an exhale. Over the last `BLOW_EARLY_S` 0.15 s at least 80% of the frames' unsmoothed rise above `levelOff` (no profile: `BLOW_ON_DB`), the smoothed rise above it too, that window's jitter at most `jitterMax`, match over `matchOff` (no profile or shapeless: coverage over `BLOW_OFF_COVERAGE` / no profile `BLOW_ON_COVERAGE`), and tone under `BLOW_MAX_TONE`. Taps (~3 frames) are still too short, voices too tonal, other noises the wrong shape.
    - Blow strength (`blow.strength`, 0–1): (rise − levelOff) ÷ the profile's `range` (no profile: `BLOW_RANGE_DB` 12).
  - **Mic check** (`MicCheck.jsx` overlay, steps in `useBreathControl`'s `stepCal`): at every Start/Restart of a session that uses the mic (Blow on the Mic, or Input Test), the mode's opening (`openMode` in `handleSelectMode`) waits behind it with the app's sound muted. Steps: wait for the mic → "stay quiet" `CAL_QUIET_S` 2 s (records the quiet rise) → `CAL_BLOWS` 3 paced breaths, like the Space Ship: **inhale** (`CAL_INHALE_S` 5 s, "Take a slow, deep breath in.", Inhale bell at × `BELL_INHALE_GAIN`, and two DOM columns at the screen's side edges whose glowing part fills bottom → top with `inhaleP`, then drains top → bottom over `CAL_EXHALE_S` 5 s from the Exhale bell, suggesting a 5 s exhale (the player may blow on past it, as in the game, and the whole exhale is recorded); eased like a ball thrown up: the fill is an ease-out (sin) that slows to a stop at the top and the drain an ease-in (cos) that starts slowly, and `MicCheck` follows `inhaleP` with a critically damped spring (`GLOW_SMOOTH_S` 0.35 s) so a fill restarting after an early blow glides instead of snapping) then **exhale** (Exhale bell at full level, "Now blow gently toward the mic, like a long, slow exhale."; waits for and records one blow: a loose-detector blow lasting ≥ `CAL_MIN_BLOW_S` 0.6 s that starts only while tone < `BLOW_MAX_TONE`, so the bell doesn't count; MicCheck cuts the bell (`stopBell`) the moment the blow starts; it ends only once the loose detector has been off `CAL_END_GRACE_S` 0.5 s, so the quiet, flickering end of a long exhale stays part of it; frames from `CAL_SKIP_S` 0.15 s to the `CAL_MAX_BLOW_S` 20 s safety cap, recorded only while the loose detector is on and the sound is still the blow: once its first `CAL_REF_S` 1 s (after `CAL_SKIP_S`) sets its shape (`makeTemplate`), a frame matching it under `CAL_SAME_BLOW_MATCH` 0.3 counts as off (a frame too flat to have a shape still counts as the blow, so the quiet end stays), so the in-breath that follows (or a voice) ends the blow instead of being learned as part of it (it was, after the end grace came in, and the polluted template stopped the ship from starting); each blow's last third is also kept as its tail (`tailExcesses`, `tailFrames`); a dot fills; after `CAL_HINT_S` 10 s without a blow a "closer / harder" hint shows). Bells go through `testBell(file, gainMult)`, straight to the output, so they're heard while the app's sound is muted → "Got it." `CAL_DONE_MS` 1.2 s → `onCalibrated` → `finishMicCheck` unmutes and runs the opening. `buildProfile`: template = the blows' mean per-band rise, mean-centred and unit length (`null`, so no shape rule, if its rms spread is under `TEMPLATE_MIN_SPREAD_DB` 1); `matchOn` = 20th percentile of the blow frames' match − 0.1 (0.2–0.75), `matchOff` = −0.15, or lower to the tails' 20th-percentile match − 0.1 (≥ 0), so a tail that turns hissier still matches; `jitterMax` = 90th percentile of the blow frames' jitter × 1.5 (3–8 dB); `levelOn` = max(quiet 95th percentile + 1.5, 0.35 × median blow rise) (3–12 dB), `levelOff` = the lower of half `levelOn` and 0.8 × the tails' 20th-percentile rise, but ≥ max(1.5, quiet 95th percentile + 0.5) and ≤ `levelOn` − 0.5, so the strict detector stays on through the user's own quiet ending but not on room noise; `range` = 90th percentile rise − levelOff (≥ 4); `toneMax` = 80th percentile tone × 1.25 + 0.05 (0.35–1.2). The profile is module-level (`blowProfile`), so **Skip mic check** keeps the previous one (or none). The check is skipped automatically when the mic fails or the control falls back to Double Sliders. App: `micCheckKey` (0 = none, a new number starts a check), `micCheckStatusRef`, `afterMicCheckRef`; Home clears it. **Self-healing mic:** no audio (below `BLOW_NO_DATA_DB` −110 dB, or the context not running) for `MIC_SILENT_MS` 1 s marks the mic stale, and the next tap (any tap, or Start/Restart) closes the context and opens a new stream and context; a new stream recalibrates. Start/Restart opens the mic after the app's sound engine has unlocked. `?micdebug` in the URL shows a readout (mic/context state, silent ms, rise dB smoothed and this frame's, % of bands risen, sustain %, jitter, tone, match, loose, early and candidate detectors, mic-check step, the profile's thresholds, status: calibrating / quiet / BLOWING / blocked by <rule> / no audio – tap to restart mic, v), plus two held lines, since the live status only flashes by: `last block:` (the last "blocked by" rule, the rise then, how long ago) and `last stop:` (the off rule that last turned the strict detector off, `blow.offWhy` / `offRise` / `offClock`, the rise then and the profile's stop level). It writes `blowRef` (1 while the mic hears a blow, else 0) for art that reacts to the blow (the Paper Airplane's glow). The mic is released when the control turns off (Home). Any tap re-requests it if the phone dropped it (lock/background). Denied or no mic → `micFailed`: same Double Sliders fallback, and the card shows "Microphone access is off, so Double Sliders will be used." Thresholds are untuned first-pass values.
- **Input Test art option** (shape id `'i'`, last Art Options card): no Morph, background or gates, only the sliders and a readout (`InputTestReadout.jsx`). `useBreathControl`'s `allSensors` turns on the tilt listener and the mic whatever the control is (motion and mic access asked in the card tap and Start/Restart), and writes `readoutRef` (`inputReadoutRef` in App) each frame: Tilt 0 flat/exhale → 1 upright/inhale, Mic = blow strength 0 quiet → 1 hard blow with BLOWING/quiet, Match = shape match with the mic check's blows (`--` without one), Tone, Slider = the breath value. `--` = no reading.
