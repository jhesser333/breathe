## Shape Option F — Hot Air Balloon (in-progress, `MorphF.jsx`)
New skin being built from scratch. Art Options card "Hot Air Balloon (in-progress)" (id `'f'`, listed after D). Camera = the Sphere Morph's placement but fov 135 (`CAMERA_BY_SHAPE.f`: `[0, 0, 12]`, level, at y 0, so the basket passes below the camera at Exhale and above it at Inhale), with `CameraVerticalShift lowerWorldUnits={4}`: the crop is moved so the z=0 plane shows 4 world units lower than the Sphere's default crop (pixels per unit from the camera's z distance and fov), leaving room above the balloon at full Inhale. Scene: the simplified balloon — a `RoundedBox` basket 0.1 square at 0,0,0 (primary color, no emissive), with the envelope sphere (primary color, emissive primary at 0.25, opacity 0.5 = the cube Morph's `EXHALE_OPACITY`, no depth write so the count pieces show through; both follow the live primary) as its child, pivot at the sphere's bottom. The basket is scaled ×9 (`BALLOON_SCALE`), which scales the whole balloon. Local (unscaled) envelope radius 0.8/3 and basket-to-envelope gap 0.3/3 (the world sizes tuned at scale 3), so at ×9 the world sizes are basket 0.9, envelope radius 2.4, gap 0.9. **Right slider** (via `leftVal` = breathRef, smoothstepped, 0 exhale/bottom → 1 inhale/top): basket position Y −8 → 6 (X/Z stay 0); envelope scale (at its bottom pivot) [0.3, 2, 0.3] → [1.5, 1, 1.25]. **Inner shell:** a second sphere in the envelope's scale group (so it scales with the envelope), same bottom pivot, radius 0.1 world larger (`SHELL_EXTRA`, world 2.5), the envelope's material but opaque with `side: BackSide` (inverted normals), so through the see-through envelope you see the inside of its far half. **Landscape:** `LandscapeF.jsx`, the balloon's own copy of `LandscapeB`, is currently **not mounted** (removed to focus on the clouds; the file is kept). No pace art, landscape, background or Morph particles yet. The left slider still drives audio. App treats `'f'` like the cube (`usesCubeFlow` in App.jsx) for the count tutorial, Slowing Down D/E hand-off and captions (cube copy for now).
- **5-breath count (`breathCountF.jsx`, balloon only):** the cube's counting engine copied unchanged (deadband tracker, slider-driven 1 s grow-in, shrink after 5, palette change, count events, secondary additive material with luminance dimming), with its own 7 sets fitted to the envelope's Inhale ellipsoid (`useBreathCountF(palette, ENVELOPE_INHALE_HALF)`, world half-extents [3.6, 2.4, 3.0]; `FIT_MARGIN` 2%). Layouts are in world units at full Inhale; MorphF's count group (at the envelope's center, inside its scale group) has scale 1 / (`BALLOON_SCALE` × `INHALE_ENVELOPE_SCALE`), so the pieces travel with the balloon and squash/stretch with the envelope. Sets, in rotation order: 1 **Nested Spheres** — `NEST_STEPS` [0.25 … 1] × the Inhale envelope shape × 0.98, smallest → largest, no spin; 2 **Cube tower** — the cube's 30°-tilted stack (gap 0.05, random Y angle, Y spin) fitted to the ellipsoid, middle three widened to the largest that fits × 0.97, slots appear in shuffled order, shrink top-down; 3 **Nested rounded cubes** — envelope-proportioned boxes (corner radius 0.15 on the unit cube), #5 the largest that fits, smallest → largest; 4 **Tetrahedrons random spawn** — circumradius `TETRA_RANDOM_R` [0.6, 1.2], random positions with the circumsphere inside the envelope (may overlap each other), all-axis tumble; 5 **Nested rings** — thick circles (`RING_TUBE` 0.1 × radius), #5 the largest whose tube stays inside at every Y angle, random order and Y angle, Y spin; 6 **Nested Tetrahedrons** — centered, #5 circumradius = the largest sphere at the center, smallest → largest, each tumbling on all axes (they cut through each other); 7 **Rounded cubes random spawn** — edge `CUBE_RANDOM_EDGE` [0.8, 1.6], bounding sphere inside the envelope, all-axis tumble. Shrink: nested sets largest first, tower top-down, others random. Spin speeds [0.3125, 0.625] rad/s.
- **Side clouds (`SideCloudsF.jsx`, every mode):** scenery like the cube's landscape. Same random clouds as the gates (`makeCloud`, 7 spheres, new per spawn), scaled [3, 1, 3] as a whole (`CLOUD_SCALE` on the cloud group, so the children scale with the parent — wide and flat), tertiary base + emissive (live), emissive 4, opacity 0.1 (`SIDE_OPACITY`), 1 s fade-in. A row each side at X ∓20, y −10, spawned at z −30 every `SPACING` 10 units per side (pre-filled to the camera at mount), removed past z 14; speed = half the gate clouds' (Slowing/Paced 10/interval, Box 3/interval, Own Pace 10/12). Slider (eased `breathRef`): X slides ±20 (Exhale) → ±10 (Inhale); each cloud reads the slider from `depthFrac × SLIDE_DELAY_S` (1 s) ago (depthFrac 0 at z 14 → 1 at z −30, from a short slider history), so the nearest move first and the wave runs front to back in both directions — the user "blows" the clouds aside. When the cloud gates start (`gatesEnabledRef`, Box Breathing/Slowing Down after the count tutorial) they all fade out over `HIDE_FADE_S` 2 s and stay hidden for the session (Own Pace keeps them).
- **Cloud gates (`cloudShapeF.js`, `GatesF.jsx`, `GatesBoxBreathingF.jsx`):** each gate is one random cloud (new per spawn): a parent sphere (unit radius, random X/Y/Z scale in `PARENT_SCALE_RANGE` [3, 4]) plus `CHILD_COUNT` 4 child spheres (each random `CHILD_SCALE_RANGE` [1, 2.5]) centered on the camera-facing (+Z) half of the parent's surface, two on −X, two on +X, then `EDGE_CHILD_COUNT` 2 more (same scale range) centered on the cloud's furthest −X point and furthest +X point (parent or child, whichever reaches furthest) — 7 spheres; scale on each mesh, not the group. Each cloud at a random X (`randomCloudX`: ±[`CLOUD_X_MIN` 0.5, `CLOUD_X_MAX` 2], never in the middle); Exhale clouds y 8, Inhale clouds y −4. Material per cloud: live text base, live secondary emissive ×4, opacity 0.5, fade-in 1 s. Judged at z = 0 with the cube's `isSuccess`: over z −0.5 → 0 (smoothstepped, held after) emissive ×1 → ×2 (the cube pulse) and opacity 0.5 → 1; **hit** — the cube's particle burst (`burstFrom` in `gateReactionsB`, same look but `CLOUD_BURST_SIZE` 120 (2× the cube's 60, `useGateBurstB`'s size arg), spawned on the cloud's ellipsoid surfaces, X-only speed × (x − the cloud's X) / outermost |x|, × outermost |x| / `CUBE_INHALE_MAX_ABS_X` 1.15 so it spreads across the cloud like the cube's spreads across a target); **miss** — emissive ramps to 0 over `MISS_RAMP_SECONDS`, no shrink, no burst. Either way opacity fades out over `FADE_OUT_S` 0.5 s, then the slot frees. `GatesF` (Slowing Down/Paced) = GatesB's timing without ties (Exhale at −20, Inhale at `computeGateBZ`, hidden until −20, `startOnInhale`, `breathPhaseRef`, `paceProgressRef`; pool 8). `GatesBoxBreathingF` = GatesBoxBreathingB's series timing (N = interval clouds per phase from −6, `onFirstGate`/`onLastGate`, `boxProgressRef`; pool 28) — the clouds overlap heavily at that spacing, kept as-is for now.
