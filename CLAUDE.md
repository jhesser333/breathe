# Breathe

## What we're building
A mobile-first React Three Fiber app where two thumb sliders drive real-time animation of a central 3D form (the Morph) as it appears to travel through an environment full of Gates. Users choose from four modes on a home screen, each offering a different breathing experience. An Art Options screen lets users choose Shape Options; Color Palette is locked to the Teal Palette for all users (no picker), and Background is fully derived from the shape choice.

## Vocabulary

**Morph** — the central animated form, fixed at position [0, 0.25, 0]. Currently a sphere (Shape Option A), rounded box (Shape Option B), dissolving sphere (Shape Options C and D — D reuses MorphC exactly but pairs it with no visible Gates/ties, see below), or concentric rotating rings (Shape Option E — flat nested rings at Exhale, gyroscope/armillary-sphere pose at Inhale; also has no visible Gates/ties, see below). Options A/B/C/D are driven by both sliders — Y scale from the right slider, X/Z scale + Fresnel from the left slider. Option E is driven only by the left slider (ring rotation state) plus the right slider (roughness only) — see MorphE below; it has no emissive/Fresnel in its current material.

**Inhale State** — left slider at top (lv=1), right slider at bottom (rv=0). Morph is tall and narrow with strong Fresnel inner glow.

**Exhale State** — left slider at bottom (lv=0), right slider at top (rv=1). Morph is wide and flat with dim emissive.

**Environment** — everything surrounding the Morph. Gates scroll past to create the illusion of forward movement.

**Road** — the invisible path along the Z-axis. Negative Z = forward (ahead of Morph); positive Z = behind (toward camera).

**Exhale Gate (Gate A)** — a single torus ring sized to frame the Morph at Exhale state (wide, flat ellipse). Spawns at z=-20.

**Inhale Gate (Gate B)** — a single torus ring sized to frame the Morph at Inhale state (narrow, tall ellipse). Spawns at z=-30 simultaneously with each Gate A, becomes visible at z=-20.

## Slider controls

**Convention: every value driven by a slider eases in and out by default.** Each Morph component reads `leftVal.current`/`rightVal.current` and immediately passes them through `THREE.MathUtils.smoothstep(v, 0, 1)` once at the top of `useFrame`, then uses those eased `lv`/`rv` for every derived lerp/scale/material property — rather than tracking the thumb's raw position 1:1. New slider-driven properties should build on the same eased `lv`/`rv`, not the raw ref values. (Detectors that need the raw, un-eased signal — e.g. `SlowingDownController`'s breath-cycle zigzag detection via `leftRawRef` — are an intentional exception, since they're reading input, not animating output.)

| Slider | Label (top/bottom) | Controls |
|--------|-------------------|----------|
| Left (0=bottom=exhale, 1=top=inhale) | inhale / exhale | X/Z scale + Fresnel inner glow intensity |
| Right (0=bottom=inhale, 1=top=exhale) | exhale / inhale | Y scale + emissive intensity |

**Left slider (lv):**
- X scale: lerp(2.2, 1.2, lv) — wide at exhale, narrow at inhale
- Z scale: lerp(0.5, 1.2, lv)
- Fresnel intensity: constant 1.0
- Fresnel power: lerp(0.2, 1.5, lv) — exhale has a much narrower, more concentrated glow (almost fully dark except a small center spot); inhale uses the wider/brighter glow that was previously the exhale mask

**Right slider (rv):**
- Y scale: lerp(3.5, 0.4, rv) — tall at inhale, flat at exhale
- Emissive intensity: piecewise — 2 at inhale (rv=0), dips to 1 at rv=0.85 (15% of the way from exhale to inhale), rises to 3 at exhale (rv=1)
- Roughness: lerp(0.3, 1, rv) — smoother at inhale, rougher at exhale

- Left slider starts at 0 (bottom / Exhale). Right slider starts at 1 (top / Exhale).
- Morph starts in Exhale state: wide flat disc.
- Slider values are refs (not state) to avoid re-renders. Updates happen in `useFrame`.
- Slider fill indicator shows progress from exhale toward inhale on both sliders.
- The left slider also exposes a raw (unclamped) ratio via `leftRawRef`, tracking the thumb's true position even past the slider's visual bounds — used by `SlowingDownController` for breath-cycle timing in "Slowing Down" mode.
- **Slider Layout**: a persisted preference (`sliderLayout`, localStorage key `sliderLayout`, values `'vertical'`/`'diagonal'`, default `'vertical'`), chosen on the **Slider Layouts** screen (see Personalization system below). `'vertical'` renders `Sliders.jsx` (unchanged, described above); `'diagonal'` renders `SlidersDiagonal.jsx` — two curved "hook" tracks (an SVG quadratic Bezier path per side, "(" on the left, ")" on the right) rendered near the bottom of the screen (`bottom: 16`, matching the Home/Restart nav), rising near-vertically from a close-together exhale end at the bottom and bowing outward to an inhale end at the top-outer corner; Home/Restart nav pinned to the bottom corners. Slider *value* semantics are identical regardless of layout (0/1 mean the same thing for `lv`/`rv` either way) — only the on-screen orientation changes. `useTouchSlider.js` takes a 3rd `orientation` param (`'vertical'` default, `'diagonal-left'`, `'diagonal-right'`) that changes how the ratio is computed from a drag point: `'vertical'` uses the single-axis `1 - (coord - start)/size` formula as before (on `clientY`/`rect.top`/`rect.height`); the two diagonal orientations normalize the raw `(clientX, clientY)` point into the track's local curve-box coordinates and call `projectToCurve` (from `diagonalCurveGeometry.js`) to find the nearest point on that side's Bezier curve, returning an arc-length fraction (0=exhale end, 1=inhale end) used as the ratio (mirrored for the right side). See `diagonalCurveGeometry.js` below for the curve math shared by rendering and hit-testing.

## Morph material
- Base color: `palette.tertiaryColor` (active Teal Palette: `#276d8c` blue-teal)
- Emissive color: `palette.primaryColor` (active Teal Palette: `#8db1a1` sage green)
- Fresnel inner glow via `onBeforeCompile` shader injection — masks `totalEmissiveRadiance` using inverse Fresnel factor, creating a center glow that fades toward edges
- Bloom post-processing (luminanceThreshold 0.2, intensity 1.5)

## Gate behavior
- **Exhale Gate (A)** spawns at z=-20 and fades in over 1s. When it passes z=0 (Morph), it triggers the next Gate A + Gate B simultaneously.
- **Inhale Gate (B)** spawns at z=-30 at the same moment as each Gate A (symmetric default — see the Slowing Down dynamic-ratio note below, which is the only place this shifts). Hidden until z=-20, then fades in.
- Encounter order: Gate A first (at morph after full interval), Gate B second (half-interval later). Sequence: A, B, A, B…
- **Gate emissive ramp** (smoothstepped): 0 at z=-3 → 1 at z=-0.5 → 2 at z=0, holds at 2 after passing. Emissive color = `palette.primaryColor`.
- **Gate alpha fade-out**: starts at z=0, complete at z=2. Smoothstepped.
- Gate color: `palette.secondaryColor` (active Teal Palette: `#0f3261` dark navy)
- Gate position Y: 0.25 (matches Morph)
- **Shape Option C**: Inhale-style torus spawns at z=-20 (self-perpetuating, same emissive ramp/fade-out as above). Additionally, a small exhale sphere (radius=0.25, diameter=0.5) spawns simultaneously at z=-30 with the same speed, hidden until z=-20 then fading in — arrives at the Morph half an interval after the inhale torus, creating alternating Inhale→Exhale→Inhale→Exhale sequence. Exhale sphere: 0.25 alpha, no persistent emissive; pulses 0→1.5 emissive intensity via `calcEmissiveExhale` (stays 0 until z=−1, single smoothstep pulse at z=0). See `GatesC.jsx`.
- **Shape Option D has no visible Gates in Paced Breathing/Slowing Down**: `GatesHeadless.jsx` reuses GatesC's exact spawn/crossing timing (same pooled-slot simulation, same alternating inhale→exhale cadence) purely to drive `breathPhaseRef` — it renders no mesh, geometry, or material, returning `null`. The breath-pacing information that Gates+ties communicate for A/B/C is instead communicated entirely by `BackgroundA`, which is forced on for this option (see Background section below). **Box Breathing is the exception** — Option D uses `GatesBoxBreathingC.jsx` directly (same visible gates as Option C) with `BackgroundA` layered on top; see the Gate geometry section below.

## Road mesh (ties) — Shape Options A–C only
Every shape option except D and E scrolls thin railroad-tie markers along the track (Options D and E have no ties — neither `GatesHeadless.jsx` nor `GatesHeadlessE.jsx` contains any tie-rendering code): `RoundedBox`, line-thin (`TIE_HEIGHT_Y`/`TIE_DEPTH_Z`/`TIE_RADIUS` all small so each tie reads as a line, not a block), at `GATE_Y`. Material: `color` = `gateColor`, no emissive, opacity = `TIE_ALPHA` (0.15) × fade-*in* only (not fade-out, so ties don't dim when a gate fades out passing the Morph) — flat, no per-position gradient.

**Pre-seeding (ties before gates enable):** Each `Gates*.jsx` pre-seeds synthetic checkpoints while `gatesEnabledRef.current` is false, so ties appear from the very first frame of Paced Breathing and Slowing Down — before any real gate ever spawns. A `preSeedRef = { elapsed, needsInitial }` tracks timing: on the first frame it pushes one checkpoint (or two for dual-gate-type options), then pushes another each time `elapsed` accumulates a full `spawnIntervalRef.current`. When gates actually enable (rising edge), pre-seed checkpoints are cleared in the same frame that `spawnA()`/`spawn()` pushes the first real checkpoint — no flicker or speed change at the transition.

Each `Gates*.jsx` keeps an independent "checkpoint" list (one entry per gate spawn — both types, for the two-gate-type options — sorted by z regardless of type, decoupled from the gate-mesh pool's own recycling) and renders three tie groups from it:
  - **Real-to-real** (`lerpRefs`/`lerpMaterials`, up to `LERP_SEGMENTS_MAX` segments × `TIES_PER_SEGMENT` (6) ties): between two consecutive real checkpoints — 1 tie at the leading gate + 5 more evenly filling the gap (frac = i/6). Both ends are real and move at their own checkpoint speed, so as gate spacing changes (e.g. Slowing Down's ramp) the tie *count* stays fixed at 6 while spacing stretches/shrinks, with no anchor-swap discontinuity.
  - **Preview** (`previewRefs`/`previewMaterials`, `TIES_PER_SEGMENT` ties): owned by the frontmost (no real gate yet ahead of it) checkpoint. Fixed offsets *ahead* of it (`frontmost.z - i * TIE_SPACING`, i=0..5), so every tie scrolls at exactly that checkpoint's own speed — no stretching toward a fixed point, hence no speed-up/jerk when the next real gate eventually spawns there and this same stretch hands off to a real-to-real segment (positions and velocities already match at that instant).
  - **Trailing filler** (`trailingRefs`/`trailingMaterials`, `TIES_PER_SEGMENT` ties): owned by the backmost (no real gate yet behind it) checkpoint. Fixed offsets *behind* it (`backmost.z + i * TIE_SPACING`); ties past `DESPAWN_Z` are hidden (so typically only 1-2 are ever visible). Skips its own i=0 (at-gate) tie when the backmost checkpoint is also the frontmost (only one real gate alive), since Preview already drew it.

  `TIE_SPACING` = the option's standard real-gate-to-real-gate distance ÷ `TIES_PER_SEGMENT`: **20/6** for the checkpoint-single option (C — ties track inhale checkpoints only; the exhale sphere doesn't push checkpoints), **10/6** for the dual-gate-type options (A, B — Exhale+Inhale spawn together 10 units apart, at `SPAWN_Z`/`GATE_B_Z`, and alternate at that steady spacing thereafter). `TIE_WIDTH_X` is sized per option below to clear the narrowest gate opening ties must pass through, inset by `TIE_GAP` so they just touch (not overlap) that edge.
  - `TIE_SPACING` stays a fixed constant even during Slowing Down's asymmetric-pace ramp (see Gate geometry sections below), even though the real `GATE_B_Z` spawn position drifts ±6.7% off -30 at the 4-6 target. Only the Preview/Trailing filler tie groups use `TIE_SPACING`; the real-to-real segment always recomputes its own spacing live from the actual gap between two real checkpoints, so this is a deliberate, visually negligible approximation on thin line-marker ties, not an oversight.

## Gate geometry (Shape Option A — GatesA.jsx)
- Base torus: radius=1.0, tube=0.06, scaled non-uniformly to match morph shape
- Exhale Gate scale: [1.229, 0.245, 1] — wide flat ellipse
- Inhale Gate scale: [0.734, 1.954, 1] — narrow tall ellipse
- Pool: 3 slots each for Gate A and Gate B
- Ties: width sized to the narrower Inhale gate's interior inner-hole half-width (`BASE_INNER * INHALE_SCALE[0]` ≈ 0.69), since the wider Exhale opening isn't the binding constraint
- **Dynamic Inhale Gate (B) spawn z**: accepts `inhaleSecondsRef`/`exhaleSecondsRef` props. `computeGateBZ()` returns `SPAWN_Z * ratio`, where `ratio = 1 + exhaleSeconds/(inhaleSeconds+exhaleSeconds)` when both refs are set, else the fixed `1.5` (identical to the module constant `GATE_B_Z = -30`). Only Slowing Down's ramp ever sets these refs — every other mode sees `ratio = 1.5`, byte-identical to before this feature existed. See Slowing Down section for the full derivation.

## Gate geometry (Shape Option B — GatesB.jsx)
- **Exhale Gate (A)**: two RoundedBox bars at X=0, args [2.8, 0.25, 0.5] — one above the morph (Y=0.65), one below (Y=-0.15)
- **Inhale Gate (B)**: two RoundedBox pillars at Y=0.25, args [0.4, 4.5, 0.5] — one left (X=-0.9), one right (X=0.9)
- Pool: 3 slots each for Gate A and Gate B
- Ties: width sized to the Inhale pillars' inner-facing edges (`GATE_B_X - CUBE_ARGS[0]/2` = 0.65) — the Exhale bars span the full X width so they don't constrain it
- **Dynamic Inhale Gate (B) spawn z**: same `computeGateBZ()` mechanism as Option A (identical helper, same `inhaleSecondsRef`/`exhaleSecondsRef` props) — `GATE_B_X`/`GATE_B_Y` (the pillars' X/Y offsets) are unrelated and unaffected; only the pillar group's Z spawn position moves.

## Gate geometry (Shape Option C — GatesC.jsx)
- **Inhale Gate (torus)**: base radius=1.0, tube=0.06, scale `[1.376, 1.955, 1]` — narrow tall ellipse sized to clear MorphC's Inhale-state half-extents. Spawns at z=−20, self-triggers next spawn when it passes z=0. Standard emissive ramp (`calcEmissive`): 0→1→2 from z=−3 to z=0.
- **Exhale Gate (sphere)**: `SPHERE_RADIUS = 0.25` (diameter 0.5, matching Option B cube block width). Spawns at z=−30 simultaneously with each inhale torus, same speed — arrives at Morph half an interval later (alternating Inhale→Exhale pattern). Hidden until z=−20, then fades in. Material: `gateColor`, 0.25 alpha, `depthWrite: false`. Emissive: `calcEmissiveExhale(z)` — stays 0 until z=−1, smoothstep pulse to peak 1.5 at z=0 (×0.75 multiplier applied in loop).
- Pool: 3 inhale slots + 3 exhale slots (independent pools)
- Ties: tracks inhale checkpoints only (exhale spheres don't push checkpoints); width sized to torus inner-hole half-width (`BASE_INNER * GATE_SCALE[0]`)
- **Dynamic Exhale sphere spawn z**: accepts `inhaleSecondsRef`/`exhaleSecondsRef` props. `computeExhaleSpawnZ()` returns `SPAWN_Z * ratio`, where `ratio = 1 + inhaleSeconds/(inhaleSeconds+exhaleSeconds)` when both refs are set, else `1.5` (identical to the module constant `EXHALE_SPAWN_Z = -30`) — note the formula is inverted relative to Options A/B (`1 + inhale/P` here vs `1 + exhale/P` there) because here the inhale gate spawns FIRST and the exhale checkpoint is SECOND. `TIE_SPACING` here only ever references `SPAWN_Z`, so it needs no dynamic counterpart.

## Gate geometry (Shape Option D — GatesHeadless.jsx, except Box Breathing)
- Morph reused directly from Option C (`MorphC.jsx`); no separate Morph file.
- **Paced Breathing, Slowing Down**: `GatesHeadless.jsx` renders no geometry at all (returns `null`) — same pooled inhale/exhale timing simulation as `GatesC.jsx` (3 inhale slots + 3 exhale slots, `SPAWN_Z=-20`/`EXHALE_SPAWN_Z=-30`, paired spawn on inhale crossing), stripped of meshes, materials, and the entire tie system. Writes `breathPhaseRef.current = 'inhale'|'exhale'` on the same z≥0 crossings GatesC would. The breath-pacing cue normally carried by visible Gates + ties is instead carried entirely by `BackgroundA` (forced on for this option — see Background section). Same dynamic `computeExhaleSpawnZ()`/`inhaleSecondsRef`/`exhaleSecondsRef` mechanism as `GatesC.jsx` (identical formula, this file's own `SPAWN_Z`).
- **Box Breathing is the exception**: Option D uses `GatesBoxBreathingC.jsx` directly — identical visible torus/sphere gates to Option C's Box Breathing, no headless substitute. `BackgroundA` still renders alongside (it's gated only on shape, not mode), so Box Breathing for Option D shows both the visible gates and the ambient background together. `breathPhaseRef` during Box Breathing is driven by `App.jsx`'s `handleBBFirstGate`/`handleBBLastGate` (via the `onFirstGate`/`onLastGate` callbacks every `GatesBoxBreathing*` component calls) rather than by the Gates component itself, so this works with zero Gates-side changes.

## Gate geometry (Shape Option E — fully headless in every mode)
- Unlike Option D, Option E has **no visible Gates in any mode, including Box Breathing** — there is no `GatesBoxBreathingE.jsx` with meshes at all.
- **Paced Breathing, Slowing Down**: `GatesHeadlessE.jsx` is an independent copy of `GatesHeadless.jsx` (own `SPAWN_Z=-20`/`EXHALE_SPAWN_Z=-30`/`DESPAWN_Z=6`/pool constants so the two can be tuned separately later) — same pooled inhale/exhale timing simulation, renders `null`, writes `breathPhaseRef`. Pacing is instead carried entirely by `BackgroundB` (concentric rings that scale with breath phase — see Background section). Same dynamic `computeExhaleSpawnZ()`/`inhaleSecondsRef`/`exhaleSecondsRef` mechanism as `GatesC.jsx`/`GatesHeadless.jsx` (identical formula, this file's own `SPAWN_Z`).
- **Box Breathing**: `GatesBoxBreathingHeadlessE.jsx` mirrors `GatesBoxBreathingC.jsx`'s exact `spawnSeries`/`onFirstGate`/`onLastGate` timing (`POOL_SIZE=28`, `SPAWN_Z=-6`, `leadZ = speed*2` pre-trigger) so App.jsx's tutorial text and `breathPhaseRef` wiring work unmodified, but renders no meshes. It additionally writes `holdFlareRef.current` (0-2) each frame: the currently-arriving series' `isFirst` slot travels from z=0 to `DESPAWN_Z` for exactly one Hold phase's duration (by construction of the spacing math), so `calcEmissive(slot.z - 3)` (the same ramp shape used by visible gates) re-centered onto that slot's `z` doubles as the Hold-phase emissive clock; `0` when no such slot is in flight (i.e. during a named Inhale/Exhale phase, not a Hold). Consumed by `StarFieldE` (see Background section) to flare the background starfield during both Hold-in and Hold-out.

## Shape D pace rings (PaceRingsD.jsx) — Box Breathing + Slowing Down
Shared hook `usePaceRings` drives three ring types, identical in both modes: **Pulse/Hold ring** (inner, `PULSE_RING_TUBE`), **Pulse/Hold Exhale ring** (outer, half the inner tube, Y-fade shader with its fade band at half height, sits at the count rings' max X scale), and **count rings** (quarter of the inner tube, X-scaling, Y-fade shader whose fade band animates with X position — full size at the inner ring, the Exhale ring's half-height band at the outer ring).
- Inhale: inner ring fades 0→alpha 0.5/emissive 1, outer fades 0.5→0, count rings shrink max X→min X. Hold-in: inner pulses once/second alpha 0.5↔1 and emissive 1↔1.25 (floor matches the movement max, so nothing jumps), outer off. Exhale/Hold-out: exact mirror (outer fades in and pulses, inner fades out and is off, count rings grow min X→max X). Count rings fade 0→0.2 alpha/emissive as they travel.
- Edge alignment via tube-ratio scale factors: count ring min (Inhale end) outer edge lines up with the Pulse/Hold ring's outer edge; count ring max X (Exhale end) inner edge lines up with the Exhale ring's inner edge. The Exhale ring itself stays placed by `LAYOUT_MIN_SCALE`/`LAYOUT_MAX_X`.
- **Startup fade-in**: when the paced art first starts (Box Breathing's first Inhale; Slowing Down D's first paced Inhale), `update()`'s `fade` multiplier (0→1, smoothstep over the first 90% of that Inhale — the same curve as the "Inhale" caption) scales every ring's alpha/emissive (Slowing Down also passes `holdExhaleRing` for the first Inhale+Exhale, pinning the Exhale ring at its movement target × fade so it fades in with the first Inhale and stays through the first Exhale instead of flashing on and off); the same value is written to App's `paceArtFadeRef` and applied by `RingParticlesD` to all three particle materials' `uGlobalFade`.
- Box Breathing: `GatesBoxBreathingD` calls `update()` from its 4-phase clock (one staggered count ring per second of hold). Slowing Down: `SlowingDownPaceRingsD` (rendered when `mode === 'slowing' && shapeOption === 'd'`) drives it from `breathPhaseRef` + `computePhaseDurations`, no holds, one count ring per phase.

## Silhouette ring (MorphC, Shape D)
A permanent, non-animated thin ring outlining the fully-inhaled Morph as seen from the camera. It sits at z=0 with scale = the inhale half-axes (1.0 × 1.5) × d/√(d² − 1) for the camera distance d=10 (≈ 1.005 × 1.508). Tube `SILHOUETTE_RING_TUBE` (the thin count-ring weight, 0.01125). Base and emissive are the live **secondary** color, emissive 1, alpha 0.1. `renderOrder` -1000 with depth test off, so everything else draws over it; it shows most during Exhale.

## Breath-count rings start point (MorphC, Shapes C/D)
The Morph's 5-breath count rings start counting at a per-mode point (`introStartsCountingRef` / `breathCountSourceRef` in App.jsx):
- **Breathe at Your Own Pace / Paced Breathing**: at the intro text hand-off, counted from the left slider (unchanged).
- **Box Breathing**: at the first Inhale of the first box cycle (the box `pendingGatesFnRef` thunk); Shape D counts from `boxProgressRef` (one ring per paced box cycle).
- **Slowing Down**: at the first paced Inhale after recording. Shapes D/E: in `startPacedArt` (see Slowing Down → D/E hand-off); Shape D counts from `ringPaceProgressRef` (one ring per paced breath). Other shapes: `PacedBreathCountStarter` watches `gatesEnabledRef` + `breathPhaseRef`.
MorphC resets its ring state whenever counting is enabled or disabled, so every start/restart begins at breath 1.
Eight sets take turns in a repeating 8-cycle pattern (`setForCycle`, `breathCycleIndexRef`): set 0 = still rings (until they fall), set 1 = rings that spin from appearance through the fall (`BREATH_SPIN_FROM_APPEAR_SET`; same material settings as the solid shapes — alpha 0.5, emissive 2 — with the Morph backlight measured against each ring's own baseline so its strength is unchanged; own depths `SPIN_RING_Z` [-45,-35,-25,-15,-5], tube 3× (`SPIN_RING_TUBE`) and 3× deeper along their own Z (`SPIN_RING_Z_SCALE`)), set 2 = **Count Cubes** (`CUBE_SET`), set 3 = **cube stack** (`CUBE_STACK_SET`), set 4 = **Count Tetrahedrons** (`TETRA_SET`), set 5 = **sphere rows** (`SPHERE_ROW_SET`), set 6 = **sphere-tetra spiral** (`SPIRAL_SET`), set 7 = **squash rings** (`SQUASH_RING_SET`). **TEMPORARY**: `TEMP_FIRST_CYCLES` = [squash rings, spiral, sphere rows] makes cycles 1–3 use those for testing. All solid sets (≥2) use alpha `CUBE_MAX_ALPHA` 0.5 and emissive `CUBE_EMISSIVE_MULT` 2.
**Rotation speeds** (all random, random direction, never near zero): all-axis spins (`makeBreathSpin`, used for spinning rings, sculpture pieces and every fall tumble) take a per-axis magnitude in `BREATH_SPIN_SPEED` [0.125, 0.3125] rad/s. Single-axis spins (`makeStackSpin` Y, `makeZSpin` Z) use `STACK_SPIN_SPEED` [0.3125, 0.625].
**Cube stack** (`layoutStack`): 5 pieces in a vertical stack on the Morph's axis, with the middle one at its center and a `STACK_GAP` 0.05 gap between pieces.
- **Tilt:** each piece is tipped `STACK_TILT` 30° about its own X axis, top away from the camera, and its Y turn is about its own tilted Y axis (Euler XYZ). Center spacing is (edge + gap)/cos 30° so the parallel tilted faces keep the `STACK_GAP` gap.
- **Size:** fitting is numeric (`pieceFits` samples 36 Y-turn angles of every corner against the Morph's full-Inhale ellipsoid). The cube edge is binary-searched so the whole all-cube stack fits, × `STACK_FIT_MARGIN` 0.97 (≈ 0.43). The top and bottom stay cubes. The middle three keep that height, but their square X/Z footprint is widened to the largest that fits (≈ 1.34 middle, ≈ 1.16 above/below).
- **Motion:** slots fill in shuffled order. Each piece has a random Y angle and spins about Y only (`makeStackSpin`). On the fall they drop bottom-up and each starts a random all-axis tumble (`breathTumbleRef`) on top of the Y turn it had reached.
**Sphere rows**: each piece is 3 spheres (radius `SPHERE_ROW_RADIUS` 0.5) in a row along X, with centers `SPHERE_ROW_SPACING` 1.5 apart (a one-radius gap between middle and outer). Pieces sit on the axis at `SPHERE_ROW_Z` [-30,-22,-16,-12,-10], farthest first, start at a random Z angle, and spin about Z only (`makeZSpin`) from appearance. When they fall they add an all-axis tumble (`breathTumbleRef`), like the cube stack.
**Squash rings**: the Pulse/Hold ring shape (count-ring torus and `BREATH_RING_SCALE`) on the axis at a shuffled depth from `SQUASH_RING_Z` [-5..-9]. Each starts at a random Z angle and spins about Z only (`makeZSpin`). Its own Y scale follows the current Z angle, `0.75 + 0.25·cos 2θ`: full when upright or upside down, `SQUASH_RING_MIN_Y` 0.5 when sideways. They tumble when they fall.
**Sphere-tetra spiral**: the sphere-row layout and depths, but the outer spheres are regular tetrahedrons, children of the center sphere, sized so their circumradius = the sphere radius (`SPIRAL_TETRA_SCALE`). Each tetrahedron has a random starting angle and tumbles on its own local axes (`spiralTetraSpinRef`) from when its piece appears. The series shares one random starting Z angle and one random Z speed (`spiralZSpinRef`). Each piece starts turning when it appears, farthest first, so the rows fan into a spiral. They tumble when they fall.
**Count Tetrahedrons**: same placement rules and motion as the Count Cubes (`placeCountCubes` with `SCULPTURE_SHAPES.tetra`: bounding sphere √(3/8)·s, disk from the average silhouette area √3/4·s²), but bigger: `TETRA_SCALE` [1.25, 2.25]. In simulation, 200 of 200 layouts were clean, with average on-screen visibility about 68% vs about 84% at the cube size range, so they overlap more. Regular tetrahedrons with rounded edges (`makeTetraGeometry`: convex hull of `TETRA_CHAMFER` 0.06 spheres at the corners, centered on the centroid).
**Count Cubes** (a growing abstract sculpture): 5 chamfered `RoundedBox` cubes in the rings' material (`CUBE_MAX_ALPHA` 0.5, emissive `CUBE_EMISSIVE_MULT` 2 vs the rings' 10). Each time the set activates, `placeCountCubes` picks a random depth (`CUBE_SPAWN_Z` [-9,-5]), size (`CUBE_SCALE` [0.75,1.5]) and starting angle for all 5. Each cube then spins slowly from the moment it appears (same appear-spin as set 1) until it falls. Rules, all spin-proof:
- **Inside the outline:** each cube's bounding sphere, seen from the camera, stays behind the Morph and inside its full-Inhale outline (`CUBE_CONTOUR_INSET` 1.0). Tested by casting camera rays through the sphere's front point and silhouette ring against the Morph ellipsoid. X/Y are derived per depth: at depth z the allowed ellipse is a·k(z) × b·k(z), with k(z) = (d − z/c)/√(d² − 1) in Morph-scaled units (Shape D: a=1, b=1.5, c=1, d=10).
- **Half visible:** cubes may intersect and overlap, but every cube keeps at least `CUBE_MIN_VISIBLE` (50%) of its on-screen disk (radius `CUBE_DISK_RADIUS`·s, the cube's average silhouette area) clear of the other cubes' disks. This is re-checked for already-placed cubes as each one is added.
- **Retries:** whole layouts retry (`CUBE_LAYOUT_TRIES`), with a best-effort fallback. In simulation, 200 of 200 layouts came out clean.

Per-object rest transforms live in `breathBaseRef`. The Morph backlight glow tracks rings only (`BREATH_RING_TOTAL`). Still-ring depths: `BREATH_RING_Z` = [-144, -55, -21, -8, -3]. At z=-3 the nearest ring's tilted edge reaches about z=-1.44, well behind the Morph's deepest extent (about z=-0.6), so X rotation doesn't clip.

## Modes

While any mode is playing, the current mode's display name (from `MODE_LABELS` in `copy.js`) appears as a small persistent uppercase caption at top-center of the experience screen (`top:16, left:'50%', transform:'translateX(-50%)'`, plain text, no background) — always on for the whole session, does not fade, distinct from `TutorialText` (`top:calc(44% + 24px)`) and `BreathLengthControl` (`top:16,left:16`).

### Basic
- Morph + environment, no Gates.
- Display label: **"Breathe at Your Own Pace"** (internal mode id remains `'basic'` everywhere in the code — only the display string changed).

### Paced Breathing
- Full experience with Gates at a fixed 12-second interval.
- Hidden from the Select Mode picker (`SelectModeScreen.jsx`'s `OPTIONS` array no longer lists it), same pattern as Shape Options A/C being hidden from the Art Options picker — the mode, its display label, its tutorial copy, and all of `App.jsx`'s `mode === 'timed'` routing stay fully implemented and reachable in code, just not selectable from the UI.
- **BreathLengthControl**: a small slider + value + label at top-left (`BreathLengthControl.jsx`) that lets the user adjust the breath interval. Hidden (opacity 0) until 2 seconds after Text D fades in, then fades in over 2 seconds. Resets to 12s and hides again when the user returns to this mode. Paced-Breathing-only — Slowing Down no longer uses it (see that section).
- **Gate spawn timing**: gates start disabled (`gatesEnabledRef.current = false`). They enable only when `showGatesText` fires (i.e., when Text C appears — after Text A and B complete, or immediately if the user skips past them). This means the road shows ties but no gate rings until Text C.

### Box Breathing
- Display label: **"Guided Breathing: Box Breathing"** (internal mode id remains `'box'` — only the display string changed, in `MODE_LABELS.box`).
- 4-phase box breath: Inhale → Hold-in → Exhale → Hold-out. Each phase duration = `spawnIntervalRef.current` seconds (default 4s).
- **Gate mechanics**: `spawnSeries(type)` spawns N=4 gates simultaneously at `SPAWN_Z=−6` with even spacing. Speed = `Math.abs(SPAWN_Z) / spawnIntervalRef.current` u/s (default 1.5 u/s). Last gate (`isLast=true`) triggers the next series when it crosses z=0; type alternates inhale↔exhale. First gate (`isFirst=true`) fires `onFirstGate(type)`; last gate fires `onLastGate(type)` — both pre-triggered at z=−speed×2 so text can start fading before the gate reaches z=0.
- **Gate files**: `GatesBoxBreathingA.jsx` (torus rings), `GatesBoxBreathingB.jsx` (cube-style), `GatesBoxBreathingC.jsx` (torus + sphere) — matched to shape options A, B, and C respectively (pool: 28 slots per file). Shape option D also uses `GatesBoxBreathingC.jsx` (same visible gates as Option C) — Box Breathing is the one mode where Option D shows visible Gates, see Gate geometry section above. Shape Option E uses `GatesBoxBreathingHeadlessE.jsx` instead — same timing, zero meshes — since Option E never shows visible Gates in any mode (see Gate geometry section above); it pairs with `BackgroundB` (rings) and `StarFieldE` (hold-phase starfield flare) for pacing.
- **No road ties** — Box Breathing does not render railroad ties.
- **Tutorial coaching sequence**: 2 full box-breath cycles then text hides. Managed by `bbCycleRef` (counts completed exhale-phase "last gate" events) and `bbTutorialActiveRef` in App.jsx. Uses `BB_TEXT_LEAD_MS = 1600ms` for `transitionBoxText` timeout (vs `FADE_TRANSITION_MS = 2000ms` used elsewhere) — ensures text starts appearing when the gate is at z=−0.6, just before the steep emissive ramp at z=−0.5 to z=0.

### Slowing Down
- Display label: **"Guided Breathing: Slowing Down"** (internal mode id remains `'slowing'` — only the display string changed, in `MODE_LABELS.slowing`).
- **Text C** fires automatically after Text B (queued via `pendingGatesFnRef` thunk in `App.jsx`). Copy: "Breathe at your own pace for a few breaths." No auto-dismiss timer — stays visible until Initial Pace is set.
- **Recording phases** (managed by `SlowingDownController.jsx`):
  - `idle` — returns early until `recordingEnabledRef.current` is true (set by `showSlowingTextC` in App.jsx when Text C appears)
  - `warmup` — counts 3 full breath cycles (min→max→min, each ≥ `MIN_BREATH_SECONDS = 1.5`); not recorded
  - `recording` — records next 2 cycles, averages them → **Initial Pace** (`avgBreathRef`); calls `onGatesReady()` callback
  - `gates` — ramps toward a fixed **Target Pace** over 60 seconds (`RAMP_SECONDS`) instead of "2× Initial Pace" — see Target Pace below; counts post-gate cycles for Text D/E
- **Target Pace** (replaces the old "ramp to 2× recorded pace" design): every user starts at their own recorded Initial Pace and ends at the same shared target, chosen up front via the **Breath Pace Options** screen (see Personalization system section) — persisted to localStorage as `targetPace` (`'5-5'` default or `'4-6'`, defined in `src/breathPace.js`'s `TARGET_PACES`). A small unlabeled **Change Target Pace** button (top-left 28×28 square matching Select Mode's Slider Layouts button, `mode === 'slowing'` only) reaches this screen mid-session; its Home button returns to `'experience'` (not `'selectMode'`, since it's reached mid-session — a deliberate deviation from every other screen's Home behavior).
  - `App.jsx` lifts two refs, `inhaleSecondsRef`/`exhaleSecondsRef`, initialized to `null` and reset to `null` unconditionally in `handleSelectMode` for every mode (plus again in `resetSlowingState`) — `null` is the sentinel every consumer (`Gates*.jsx`, `BackgroundA`/`BackgroundB`) treats as "no split, use the symmetric 1.5 ratio default." This prevents a stale asymmetric split from leaking into a shared `GatesComponent` instance if the user switches from a mid-ramp Slowing Down session into Paced Breathing.
  - `SlowingDownController.jsx`'s `gates`-phase block (replacing the old "×(1+t)" ramp) computes, every frame: `t = clamp((now - phase2StartRef)/RAMP_SECONDS, 0, 1)`, `startHalf = avgBreathRef/2`, then linearly interpolates `inhaleSecondsRef.current` from `startHalf` → `targetInhaleSeconds` and `exhaleSecondsRef.current` from `startHalf` → `targetExhaleSeconds`, and sets `spawnIntervalRef.current = inhaleSecondsRef.current + exhaleSecondsRef.current`. No "ramp done" guard is needed — this is idempotent once `t=1`, so it runs unconditionally for the rest of the session.
  - For the **5-5** target, `inhaleSecondsRef` and `exhaleSecondsRef` stay identical at every `t` (same start, same target) — the derived gate-spawn ratio stays exactly 1.5 throughout, so this option requires zero change to any Gate/Background file's ratio math, only the overall period (`spawnIntervalRef`) ramps toward a fixed 10 instead of `avgBreath × 2`.
  - For the **4-6** target, the split genuinely diverges, and each `Gates*.jsx`/`BackgroundA`/`BackgroundB` file uses the live `inhaleSecondsRef`/`exhaleSecondsRef` values to compute an asymmetric spawn-distance ratio (see the Gate geometry sections above and Background section below) — the exhale phase becomes visibly longer than the inhale phase as the ramp progresses.
  - Changing the target pace mid-ramp lets the interpolation redirect toward the new target for the remaining ramp time (or snap immediately if the ramp already finished) — not special-cased, just the natural consequence of the architecture.
- **D/E hand-off (Shapes D and E only)**: recording done does NOT start the art. `handleSlowingRecordingDone` sets `phase2StartRef = Infinity` (ramp holds at the recorded pace), fades Text C, then `showPacedText('D')` shows Text D ("You will soon see art animate at the rate you recorded."), followed by Text E ("Keep your breathing in sync with the art as it slows down."). Each is timed exactly like the Diagonal intro's Text B2: 1 s fade-in (`DIAG_FADE_IN_MS`), hold for one left-slider breath cycle (`updatePacedText`, called from `setLeft`: `DIAG_CYCLE_STROKES` reversals with `DIAG_DEADBAND`, stage in `pacedTextStageRef`), 2 s fade-out (`DIAG_FADE_OUT_MS`). The controller's `onTextDone`/`onTextEDone` are no-ops for D/E. After Text E fades, `pacedWaitForBottomRef` arms; `setLeft` calls `startPacedArt()` once the left slider is at the bottom (`<= DIAG_EDGE_THRESHOLD`), which starts the ramp clock, starts count-ring counting, and enables gates. `GatesHeadless`/`GatesHeadlessE` get `startOnInhale` (true in Slowing Down): on enable they write `breathPhaseRef='inhale'` immediately and spawn an extra exhale checkpoint at `SPAWN_Z * inhale/P` so the first Exhale lands `inhaleSeconds` later. `PacedPhaseWatcher` reports each phase change to `handlePacedPhase`, which shows Box-style "Inhale"/"Exhale" captions for the first 5 breaths (`TutorialText` `pulseMode='paced'` + `pacedCaptionRef`: whole-phase fade × `PACED_CAPTION_ALPHA` [1,1,1,0.5,0.15]); nothing follows the captions. From Text E on, idle re-show falls back to Text E. The phase-locking below applies to Shapes A/B/C only.
- **Gate phase-locking**: when recording ends, gates are NOT enabled immediately. A delay `d` is computed so the first gate arrives at the Morph on the user's next inhale peak:
  - `lastMaxTimeRef` (ref passed from App.jsx) stores the exact timestamp of the last confirmed inhale peak
  - Single-gate shapes (C, D, E — GatesHeadless/GatesHeadlessE mirror GatesC's timing, spawn z=−20, travel time = 1 interval): `d = P − elapsed_since_max`
  - Dual-gate shapes (A/B, Gate B spawn z=−30, travel time = 1.5 intervals): `d ≈ 0` (Gate B naturally aligns)
  - `gateEnableTimerRef` fires after `d` seconds to set `gatesEnabledRef.current = true`
  - `phase2StartRef` is set to gate-enable time so the 60s ramp clock starts from the first visible gate
  - This math's fixed `1.5 * P` `T_travel` assumption (`App.jsx`) relies on `inhaleSecondsRef`/`exhaleSecondsRef` still being exactly `avgBreathRef/2` each (ratio 1.5) at `t=0` of the ramp — true regardless of the selected target pace, since the ramp always starts from that symmetric point.
- **Text D** fades in 2 seconds after Text C fades. Copy: "Good Job! The oncoming targets will begin at your pace and slow down over the next minute." No auto-dismiss — stays until user completes `TEXT_D_CYCLES = 3` post-gate breath cycles, then fades. `SlowingDownController` calls `onTextDone()` callback.
- **Text E** fades in 2 seconds after Text D fades. Copy: "Keep Morph aligned with the targets to slow down your breathing." No auto-dismiss — stays until user completes `TEXT_E_CYCLES = 4` more post-gate breath cycles, then fades. `SlowingDownController` calls `onTextEDone()` callback.
- **No post-ramp text or UI**: unlike the old design, nothing further appears once the ramp reaches its target — no Text F/G, no `BreathLengthControl`. The session simply continues at the target pace (or whatever the user last selected via Breath Pace Options) with the mode-label caption and the "Change Target Pace" button as the only persistent UI.
- Constants in `SlowingDownController.jsx`: `DEADBAND=0.08`, `MIN_BREATH_SECONDS=1.5`, `SLACK_FACTOR=1.15`, `WARMUP_CYCLES=3`, `RECORD_CYCLES=2`, `TEXT_D_CYCLES=3`, `TEXT_E_CYCLES=4`, `RAMP_SECONDS=60`

## Tutorial text rules
Universal A/B sequence, then mode-specific C/D (defined in `src/copy.js`). **This A/B sequence is Vertical-slider-layout only** — the Diagonal layout uses a completely different Text A1/A2/B1/B2 sequence instead (see below); both hand off to the same mode-specific Text C onward.

- **Text A** (Vertical layout only) — "Move the sliders / in opposite directions / with your thumbs" (3 lines via `\n`). Shown at mode start. Stays visible until the **right slider** has completed **2 full up+down oscillations** (4 direction reversals detected with an 8% deadband, tracked in `rightStrokeCountRef`), then fades out over 2 seconds. If the user never moves the right slider, Text A stays up indefinitely.
- **Text B** (Vertical layout only) — "The transforming object is named Morph. / Sync your breathing to Morph." (line break after "Morph." via `\n`). Fades in once Text A fades out. Stays visible until the right slider completes **3 more full oscillations** (6 reversals, same deadband logic, counters reset when B begins), then fades out over 2 seconds.
- **Diagonal layout Text A1/A2/B1/B2** (replaces Text A/B entirely for `sliderLayout === 'diagonal'`; driven by `updateDiagonalSequence` in `App.jsx`, called from `setLeft`, using `diagStageRef` as the stage cursor):
  - **Text A1** — "Inhale and move the sliders up." Shown immediately at mode start, opacity bound continuously to `1 - lv` (left slider) via a numeric `opacity` prop on `TutorialText` — no fixed timer, it fades exactly in step with the left slider moving from bottom (0) toward top (1). When `lv >= 1 - DIAG_EDGE_THRESHOLD` (0.98), Text A1 is effectively invisible and Text A2 begins.
  - **Text A2** — "Good! Exhale and move the sliders down." Fades in over 1 second (`DIAG_FADE_IN_MS`) from 0, then tracks continuously: opacity = `lv`, fading out 1→0 as the user drags back down. When `lv <= DIAG_EDGE_THRESHOLD` (0.02), Text A2 is effectively invisible and Text B1 begins.
  - **Text B1** — "Wonderful! Keep moving the sliders with your breath." Fades in over 1 second. Once fully visible, stays up for **1 full breath cycle** — detected as 2 left-slider direction reversals ("strokes", `DIAG_CYCLE_STROKES = 2`, same 8%-deadband algorithm as the Vertical layout's oscillation counter, tracked in `diagDirectionRef`/`diagExtremeRef`/`diagReversalCountRef`) — then fades out over 2 seconds (`DIAG_FADE_OUT_MS`).
  - **Text B2** — "Notice how your breathing transforms the art." Fades in over 1 second once Text B1 has fully faded out. Stays up for 1 more full breath cycle (same 2-stroke detection, counters reset), then fades out over 2 seconds.
  - After Text B2 fully fades out, `diagStageRef.current` is set to `'done'` and — exactly like the Vertical layout's Text B → Text C handoff — `pendingGatesFnRef.current` (if set) is consumed and invoked, starting the mode-specific Text C onward. Basic mode leaves `pendingGatesFnRef` null, so the tutorial simply ends, same as Vertical.
  - The 10-second idle-reshow effect is suppressed while this sequence is in progress (`sliderLayout === 'diagonal' && diagStageRef.current !== 'done'`) since the continuous-opacity phases already reflect live slider position rather than a hidden/shown boolean; it resumes normally once the sequence reaches `'done'`, covering Text C onward exactly as it does for Vertical.
  - `TutorialText.jsx` accepts optional `opacity` (0–1 number) and `fadeMs` (number, default 2000) props: when `opacity` is a number it overrides the `visible` boolean entirely (used for A1/A2's continuous fades), otherwise `visible` + a CSS `opacity {fadeMs}ms ease` transition is used (used for A2/B1/B2's fixed fade-in/fade-out steps, and unchanged for all of Vertical's Text A/B onward).
- **Text C (Paced Breathing)** — "Fit Morph through the oncoming targets to pace your breath." Queued via `pendingGatesFnRef` thunk when Paced Breathing mode starts. Fires after Text B finishes (or immediately if A/B already done). Enables gates. Fades out after **2 breath cycles** (`spawnIntervalRef × 2` seconds, captured at Text C fire time).
- **Text D (Paced Breathing)** — "Adjust the pace of the targets with the slider on the left." Fades in 2 seconds after Text C fades (FADE_TRANSITION_MS gap). `BreathLengthControl` fades in 2 seconds after Text D appears. Fades out after **3 breath cycles** (`spawnIntervalRef × 3` seconds).
- **Shape Options D/E ambient copy**: since Options D and E have no visible targets, `showGatesText` (Paced Breathing Text C/D) and `showSlowingTextD`/`showSlowingTextE` (Slowing Down Text D/E) branch on `shapeRef.current === 'd' || shapeRef.current === 'e'` in `App.jsx` and substitute `TEXTS.gatesTimedAmbient` / `TEXTS.gatesTimedDAmbient` / `TEXTS.slowingTextDAmbient` / `TEXTS.slowingTextEAmbient` (in `copy.js`), which reference the background's rhythm instead of targets — Option E reuses these verbatim (no dedicated ring-specific copy yet). All other tutorial text (A, B, Slowing Down C/D/E, Box Breathing C–F) is shape-agnostic and unchanged for Options D/E.
- **Text C (Slowing Down)** — "Breathe at your own pace for a few breaths." Queued via `pendingGatesFnRef` thunk when Slowing Down mode starts. Fires after Text B finishes. Sets `recordingEnabledRef.current = true` to start the warmup/recording flow in `SlowingDownController`. No auto-dismiss timer — stays until `SlowingDownController` calls `onGatesReady()` (recording complete), which triggers Text C fade-out.
- **Text D (Slowing Down only)** — "Good Job! The oncoming targets will begin at your pace and slow down over the next minute." (Options D/E: "You will soon see art animate at the rate you recorded." — B2-style timing, followed by Text E; see Slowing Down → D/E hand-off) Shown 2 seconds after Text C fades out (after FADE_TRANSITION_MS gap). No auto-dismiss timer — stays until `SlowingDownController` calls `onTextDone()` after 3 post-gate breath cycles, then fades.

(Options D/E: "Keep your breathing in sync with the art as it slows down." — shown right after Text D with the same B2-style timing, before the art starts; the idle re-show text from then on) Shown 2 seconds after Text D fades. Pacing is carried entirely by `BackgroundB` (concentric rings) plus, in Box Breathing only, `StarFieldE` (see Background section).

**Background:**
- No user-facing picker — `backgroundOption` is a derived constant in `App.jsx`, not stored state: `shapeOption === 'e' ? 'b' : shapeOption === 'd' ? 'a' : 'none'`. Shape Options A/B/C always render no background; Shape Option D always renders `BackgroundA`, Shape Option E always renders `BackgroundB`, in every mode including Box Breathing (the derivation is shape-only, not mode-dependent). This exists because A/B/C already communicate breath pacing via visible Gates + ties, and layering the animated background on top of those was judged too visually noisy — Options D and E remove the Gates/ties in every mode and rely on the background as their pacing cue; in Box Breathing, Option D restores the visible gates (see Gate geometry section) and the background layers on top as a supplementary cue, while Option E stays fully headless even in Box Breathing and adds `StarFieldE` as its Box-Breathing-only supplementary cue instead.
- **`BackgroundA`**: 30 scattered RoundedBox cubes (same geometry as GatesB inhale cubes, args [0.5, 0.5, 0.5], radius 0.1) randomly distributed across X∈[−8, 8], Y∈[−10, −4], Z∈[−30, 5]. Material: `gateColor` / `morphEmissive`, transparent. Animate between **State A** (opacity=0, emissiveIntensity=0, Y offset −5 from spawn) and **State B** (opacity=0.1, emissiveIntensity=1, at spawn Y) driven by `breathPhaseRef` + `gatesEnabledRef`: State B during exhale phase (exhale gate crosses z=0), State A during inhale phase. Animation progresses toward State B (target=1) over `exhaleSecondsRef.current` seconds and toward State A (target=0) over `inhaleSecondsRef.current` seconds — direction-aware, so Slowing Down's asymmetric-pace ramp (see that section) is reflected here too; falls back to `spawnIntervalRef.current/2` for both directions (byte-identical to the old single-halfInterval behavior) whenever either ref is `null`, which is every mode except Slowing Down mid/post-ramp. Smoothstep-eased. Cubes remain hidden while `gatesEnabledRef.current` is false — invisible in Basic mode and before Text C in other modes.
- **`BackgroundB`** (Shape Option E only): 5 concentric torus rings (default/unrotated orientation, facing the camera like Gates torus meshes), sharing one material (`color: gateColor`, **no emissive**, `transparent`, `opacity: 0.6`), positioned in a group at `[0, 0.25, -2]` with radii stepped 0.6→2.4. Unlike `BackgroundA`'s per-mesh opacity/Y animation, the entire group's `scale` lerps 0→1 (0 at Inhale, full size at Exhale, matching the concept art), reusing `BackgroundA`'s exact `progressRef`/`target`/direction-aware-`halfInterval`/local-smoothstep timing logic (driven by the same `breathPhaseRef`/`gatesEnabledRef`/`spawnIntervalRef`/`inhaleSecondsRef`/`exhaleSecondsRef` props). The outer rings are expected to bleed past the screen edges on a portrait phone at full scale — intentional, matching the reference art — but the starting radius/position constants are a first pass meant to be tuned on-device.
- **`StarFieldE`** (Shape Option E, Box Breathing only): ~50 small always-visible spheres (radius 0.045) scattered across X∈[−8,8], Y∈[−3,6], Z∈[−30,5] (a different Y range than `BackgroundA`'s scatter, chosen because these stars have no reveal animation and need to already sit in view). Material matches the gate-material convention (`color: gateColor`, `emissive: emissiveColor`), sharing one instance across all stars whose `emissiveIntensity` is set every frame from `holdFlareRef.current` (0-2, written by `GatesBoxBreathingHeadlessE` — see Gate geometry section above). Intensity-only flare, no hue change — matches how the real gates actually behave despite reading as a "color change."

## Current scene setup
- **Camera**: per-shape, via the `CAMERA_BY_SHAPE` map in `App.jsx` (keyed by `shapeOption`, falling back to `DEFAULT_CAMERA` = Option A's entry). Shape Options A/B/C: position `[0, 3.5, 5]`, fov 50, **no `rotation` key** — R3F's default look-at-origin supplies the ~35° downward angle (dropping the key is what produces the tilt, so don't add `rotation: [0, 0, 0]` back). Shape Option D: position `[0, 0, 10]`, `rotation: [0, 0, 0]`, fov 50 — level and pulled back, plus `CameraVerticalShift` (D-only shift-lens crop, see below). Shape Option E: position `[0, 0, 5]`, `rotation: [0, 0, 0]`, fov 50 — level. R3F applies the camera prop at Canvas mount; `shapeOption` only changes from the Art Options screen, which unmounts the Canvas, so each option always gets a fresh camera.
- **`CameraVerticalShift.jsx`** (Shape Option D only): shift-lens vertical crop via `camera.setViewOffset` — renders a frame `EXTRA_HEIGHT_FRACTION` (0.6) taller than the viewport and shows a sub-window offset downward by `SHIFT_FRACTION` (0.65) of that headroom plus `EXTRA_SHIFT_PX` (24px ≈ ¼"), so the scene sits higher in frame without rotating or moving the camera in world space.
- **Background**: from active palette (Teal Palette: `#1a1a3a` dark blue)
- **Lights**: ambientLight 0.4 + directionalLight at `[5, 5, 5]` intensity 1
- **Morph**: sphere radius 0.5, position [0, 0.25, 0], scaled via group
- **Bloom**: `@react-three/postprocessing` v2, luminanceThreshold=0.2, luminanceSmoothing=0.9, intensity=1.5

## Axis orientation
- **X** — width (left/right)
- **Y** — height (up/down)
- **Z** — depth along road (negative Z = ahead; positive Z = toward camera)
- Morph at [0, 0.25, 0]; gates spawn at z=-20 (A) or z=-30 (B), travel toward z=0
